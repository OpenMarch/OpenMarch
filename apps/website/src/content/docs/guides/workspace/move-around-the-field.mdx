## With a mouse

- The scroll wheel on its own pans the field up and down.
- To pan in any direction, drag with the right mouse button, or with the middle button (the scroll wheel pressed down). On a Mac, you can also hold <Kbd keys="Ctrl" gesture="drag" /> and drag.
- To zoom, hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="scroll" /> and turn the scroll wheel. The field zooms around the pointer, so point at what you want to see.

A right-click that doesn't move opens the field's menu instead of panning.

To make the wheel zoom without holding a key, turn off <Ui k="settings.mouse.trackpadMode" /> in <Ui k="settings.title" /> › <Ui k="settings.mouse" />. [Settings](/guides/reference/settings/#mouse--trackpad) has the zoom and pan speeds too.

## With a trackpad

- To pan, scroll with two fingers in any direction.
- To zoom, pinch with two fingers, or scroll with <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="scroll" />.

## With the zoom controls

The zoom controls float at the bottom of the field.

<Media
  id="workspace-zoom-controls"
  alt="The zoom controls: a slider above a minus button, the zoom percentage and a plus button."
  maxWidth={260}
/>

- Drag the slider, or select the minus and plus buttons, to zoom around the middle of the view.
- Select the percentage to fit the whole field in view again. 100% is always the whole field fitted to the space it has, so the number means the same thing however big the window is.

When the window or the timeline changes size, the field keeps its zoom and stays where you left it; select the percentage to fit it to the new size.

## On a tablet

Drag with two fingers to pan, and pinch to zoom. One finger on empty field pans too, unless you've set the field's drag mode to select instead. [Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/#move-the-view-with-two-fingers) has the rest of the touch rules.

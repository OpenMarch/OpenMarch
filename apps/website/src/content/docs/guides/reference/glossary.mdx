The words this guide uses, in alphabetical order. Each one means the same thing on every page.

## 3D View

A window that shows the show from the stands, with marchers in uniform on a field in a stadium. You watch in it; you write drill in the editor. See [Open the 3D View](/guides/3d-view/open-the-3d-view/).

## 8 to 5

A step size: eight steps for every five yards, so each step is 22.5 inches. Most drill is written in 8 to 5. A larger step size, such as 6 to 5, covers the same distance in fewer, longer steps.

## Arc

A shape that places marchers along part of a circle. See [Draw an arc](/guides/shapes/draw-an-arc/).

## Back sideline

The sideline farthest from the audience. Its opposite is the front sideline.

## Basic

The editor mode that works in pages, like a drill chart: you set each page, and marchers hold until you move them again. See [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

## Box

A shape that places marchers in rows and columns, given as across × deep, such as 8 × 4. See [Draw a box](/guides/shapes/draw-a-box/).

## Count

One beat of the show's music. Moves, pages and the playhead all fall on counts.

## Defined

A marcher's state on a page where you've set its position: a move of its own ends there, at a keyframe. See [Defined, Held, Not defined](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/).

## Drill number

A marcher's label on the field and in charts, such as T3 or F12: the section's prefix and a number.

## Editor

The view for making changes to the show. Its opposite is Preview.

## Expert

The editor mode with the full timeline: moves that cross pages, overlapping moves, the start flag and the loop. See [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

## Field

The playing surface and its markings: sidelines, yard lines, hashes and numbers. Each show has its own field, which you can change. See [Edit the field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/).

## Front sideline

The sideline nearest the audience. Front and back on the field are measured from it.

## Hash

A row of short marks across each yard line that divides the field front to back. Where the hashes sit depends on the kind of field: high school, college and pro fields put them at different distances from the sidelines.

## Held

A marcher's state on a page where you've chosen to keep it still, with <Ui k="timeline.terms.holdFromPrevious" /> or <Ui k="timeline.terms.holdOverWindow" />. A hold grows out of the keyframe where the marcher last arrived, or its starting spot. If you change that keyframe, it holds at the new spot, and dragging the marcher inside the hold moves the whole hold. See [Defined, Held, Not defined](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/).

## Home

The start of the show, before the first move: where every marcher stands at the beginning. It's the set of the first page.

## Inspector

The panel at the side of the window that shows the details of what you've selected: the page, or the selected marchers and their positions.

## Keyframe

A position you set for a marcher at a count. Marchers move from one keyframe to the next.

## Line

A shape that places marchers evenly along a straight line. See [Draw a line](/guides/shapes/draw-a-line/).

## Loop

In Expert, a stretch of the show that playback repeats while you work on it. See [Play the show](/guides/timeline/play-the-show/).

## Marcher

Anyone on the field, in any section, including the color guard. The guide doesn't say performer or dot.

## Measure

A group of counts in the music, as numbered in the score. The timeline shows measure numbers above the counts.

## Move

A stretch of time on the timeline in which marchers go from one place to another. A page usually has one move across it; in Expert, a move can also start or end in the middle of a page, or run across several pages. See [Moves](/guides/timeline/moves/).

## Not defined

A marcher's state on a page where nothing has been decided for it. It shows the last position you set, and moves with any edit to that position. See [Defined, Held, Not defined](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/).

## Page

A numbered stretch of counts that ends at a set the marchers reach. Pages are numbered as on a drill chart. See [Pages](/guides/timeline/pages/).

## Playhead

The line on the timeline that marks the count you're looking at. The field shows where every marcher is at that count.

## Preview

The view for watching the show without changing it. Its opposite is Editor. See [Editor and Preview](/guides/workspace/editor-and-preview/).

## Prop

An object on the field, such as a riser or a backdrop, that you draw and move on the timeline like marchers. See [Props](/guides/3d-view/props/).

## Rehearsal mark

A letter or name for a place in the music, such as A or Letter B, shown with the measures. You can go to one by name.

## Section

A group of marchers by instrument or role, such as Trumpet or Color guard. Each section has a prefix for its drill numbers.

## Set

Where the marchers stand at the end of a page: the picture a drill chart shows for that page.

## Shape

A box, line or arc you draw on the field to place the selected marchers in it. See [Shapes](/guides/shapes/).

## Show

One `.dots` file: its marchers, music, field and drill.

## Side 1 and Side 2

The two halves of the field, split at the 50 yard line. Side 1 is on the audience's left and Side 2 on the right, unless the show's field names them differently.

## Snap

Moving marchers and shape points onto the nearest step, yard line or hash as you drag. See [Snapping and modifier keys](/guides/shapes/snapping-and-modifier-keys/).

## Start flag

In Expert, the flag on the timeline that marks where playback starts.

## Step size

How far a marcher travels on each count, given as steps per five yards, such as 8 to 5. OpenMarch can mark moves whose steps are too large to march. See [Step size warnings](/guides/field/step-size-warnings/).

## Subset

A page added inside another page and numbered after it with a letter, such as 2A after page 2, so the pages after it keep their numbers.

## Tempo

The speed of the music, in beats per minute. A show can change tempo, all at once or gradually. See [Tempo](/guides/music/tempo/).

## Timeline

The strip along the bottom of the window that holds the show's counts, measures, pages, moves and music, with the playback controls. See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/).

## Yard line

One of the lines across the field every five yards, numbered from each goal line to the 50. Drill positions are often given as steps from the nearest yard line.

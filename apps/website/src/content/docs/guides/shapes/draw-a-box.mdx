<InShort>Select the marchers, press <Kbd action="shapeBlock" />, drag on the field from one corner of the box to the other, then select <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" /> (<Kbd action="applyShape" />).</InShort>

**Before you start:** select the marchers who go in the box, and put the <Term id="playhead">playhead</Term> on the count where they should stand in it, usually the end of a move.

<Steps>

1. In the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.shapesTab" /> tab, select <Ui k="shapes.kinds.block.label" />, or press <Kbd action="shapeBlock" />.

2. Drag on the field from one corner of the box to the opposite corner. A marcher stands on the corner you pressed, and the box picks how many across suit the shape you drag.

3. Change the box if you need to: drag its handles, or use its floating editor (below).

4. Select <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" />, or press <Kbd action="applyShape" />.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="shapes-draw-box"
  alt="B is pressed, and a drag on empty field beside a row of trumpets draws a 4 × 3 box of spots. The pointer moves to Place and clicks it, and the trumpets move onto the spots."
/>

The marchers take their spots, as one step you can undo. Until you place it, nobody moves, and <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> throws the box away. On a tablet, drag with one finger, and select <Ui k="shapes.edit.discard" /> beside <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" /> to throw it away.

## Change the box on the field

- Drag a side or corner handle to stretch the box. The rows stay the same and the spacing between them changes.
- Drag inside the box, or the dot in its middle, to move it.
- Drag the round knob to turn it, in 15° steps.

The handles, knob and editor fade while the pointer is away from the box, so you can see the field under them.

## Change the box's numbers

The <Term id="floating-editor">floating editor</Term> under the box holds its numbers and buttons. To change a number, click it and type, then press <Kbd keys="Enter" />, or drag it. [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/#spots-intervals-and-steps) says how spacings can be typed.

<Media
  id="shapes-box-editor"
  alt="The floating editor of an open box. First row: minus, 4 × 3, plus; the side-to-side spacing 9 and front-to-back spacing 7½ steps with a chain button; a turn button, 0°, and the pivot menu; two flip buttons, Shrink, Expand and Recalculate; an eye, a magnet and a pathways button; and Place. Second row: Fill set to Grid and Layout set to Full."
/>

From left to right, the first row has:

- The size, such as 4 × 3: how many across and how many deep. − and + give one fewer or one more across. Drag the number sideways to change how many across, or up and down for how many deep.
- The side-to-side and front-to-back spacing, in steps. The chain button locks the two together, so changing one changes the other in proportion.
- ↻ turns the box 90° clockwise (<Kbd action="shapeQuarterTurn" />), and the degrees beside it turn it to any angle. The pivot menu picks the point it turns and flips around: a corner, the middle of a side or the center.
- The two flip buttons mirror it side to side (<Kbd action="flipHorizontal" />) or front to back (<Kbd action="flipVertical" />).
- Shrink and Expand make the box smaller or bigger a half step at a time, keeping its proportions. Hold either to repeat it.
- <Ui k="shapes.edit.recalculate.label" /> works out [who goes where](/guides/shapes/#who-goes-where) again (<Kbd action="shapeRecalculate" />).
- The eye hides the marchers and spots, so you see the outline (<Kbd action="shapeHideMarchers" />). The magnet turns [snapping](/guides/shapes/snapping-and-modifier-keys/#turn-snapping-off) off. The last button shows each marcher's path to their spot.

## Choose how the box is filled

The <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.label" /> menu on the editor's second row has three choices:

<Media
  id="shapes-fills"
  alt="An open 4 × 3 box of twelve trumpets in Grid. The Fill menu is opened and Outline chosen: the marchers move to the box's edge, a marcher on every corner and the middle empty. Then Spread is chosen, and they scatter evenly over the whole box in no rows."
/>

| Fill                                 | What it does                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.files" plain />   | In rows, across and deep. The <Ui k="shapes.edit.layout.label" plain /> menu beside it lays the block out <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.full" plain />, <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.stagger" plain />, <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.rounded" plain />, <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.shortBack" plain /> or <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.notched" plain />, and says how many marchers to add or remove for a layout that can't take this many. |
| <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.outline" plain /> | Round the edge, with a marcher on every corner and the middle empty. When the count doesn't go round evenly, the Leftover menu picks the side that has one fewer marcher, and whether it leaves a gap or spreads its marchers wider. <Ui k="shapes.edit.evenOut.label" plain /> spaces everyone as evenly as the box allows. |
| <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.spread" plain />  | Evenly over the whole box, in no rows. <Ui k="shapes.edit.reshuffle.label" plain /> gives another even layout in the same box; the arrow beside it shows the layout's <Ui k="shapes.edit.seed.label" plain />, which you can copy, or type back in to get that exact spread again. <Ui k="shapes.edit.edge.label" plain /> stands some marchers along the outline and spreads the rest inside. |

In <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.files" plain />, the <Ui k="shapes.edit.layout.label" plain /> menu shows a small picture of each layout, and beside it the sizes that layout can make for your marchers:

<Media
  id="shapes-layout-menu"
  alt="The floating editor of an open 4 × 3 box in Grid, with its Layout menu open: Full, Stagger, Rounded, Short back and Notched, each with a small picture of the block. Notched is greyed out and reads add 1 / remove 2. Beside the list, Full offers two sizes for these twelve marchers, 6 × 2 and 4 × 3, with 4 × 3 picked."

/>

With <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.shortBack" plain />, when the marchers don't fill the last row, the size says how many spots are short.

Switching fills keeps the box where you drew it, and each fill remembers its own numbers, so switching back gives you what you had.

## Type a box instead

<Steps>

1. With the marchers selected, press <Kbd action="openCommandBar" /> or <Kbd action="openCommandPalette" /> to open the <Term id="command-palette">command palette</Term>.

2. Type "box", then press <Kbd keys="Enter" />.

3. Answer each question: the fill, the size (such as 6x4), the side-to-side spacing, the front-to-back spacing and the layout. Each answer starts with a default, so <Kbd keys="Enter" /> keeps it. The box shows on the field as you type.

</Steps>

The last answer places the box. You can also type it in one go: "box 6x4 2" is a box 6 across and 4 deep, 2 steps apart. <Kbd keys="Backspace" /> in an empty answer goes back a question, and <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> cancels.

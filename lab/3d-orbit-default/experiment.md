Open the **3D View** from the **View** tab.

**What to try**

- Drag anywhere, including on the stands and the press box, to orbit.
- Drag with the right mouse button, or hold Shift while you drag, to move across the field.
- Scroll or pinch to zoom toward the pointer.
- If you'd rather drag to move across the field, pick **Map** under **Controls** in **View settings** (the gear button in the 3D View).

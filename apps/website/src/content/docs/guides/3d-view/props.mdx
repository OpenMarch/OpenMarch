<InShort>Put the playhead where the prop first appears, select <Ui k="props.title" /> (the cube) in the sidebar, choose an outline such as <Ui k="props.outline.rectangle" plain />, and draw it on the field.</InShort>

A prop is a set piece on the field: a riser, a platform, a floor tarp, a wall or a backdrop. You draw its outline on the field, give it a height, and move it during the show the way you move marchers. The 3D View draws each prop as a plain block.

The pit's instruments, speakers and podiums aren't props: see [Set up the front ensemble](/guides/3d-view/front-ensemble/).

## Draw a prop

<Steps>

1. Move the playhead to the count where the prop first appears on the field.

2. Select <Ui k="props.title" /> (the cube) in the sidebar.

3. Under <Ui k="props.draw.title" plain />, choose an outline: <Ui k="props.outline.rectangle" />, <Ui k="props.outline.circle" />, <Ui k="props.outline.arc" />, <Ui k="props.outline.polygon" /> or <Ui k="props.outline.freehand" />. The panel closes and a note at the bottom of the field says how to draw that outline.

4. Draw the outline on the field:
   - <Ui k="props.outline.rectangle" plain />: drag from one corner to the opposite corner.
   - <Ui k="props.outline.circle" plain />: drag from the center out to the edge.
   - <Ui k="props.outline.arc" plain />: click both ends of the arc, then click to set how far it curves.
   - <Ui k="props.outline.polygon" plain />: click each corner, then double-click the last one to finish.
   - <Ui k="props.outline.freehand" plain />: drag along the shape you want.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-props-draw"
  alt="Rectangle is chosen in the Props panel, the panel closes and a note at the bottom of the field says to drag, then a drag in the open field behind the band draws a rectangle prop about ten yards wide."
/>

The prop appears as soon as you finish, and the drawing tool turns off. To stop drawing without making a prop, select <Ui k="props.draw.cancel" /> in the note or press <Kbd keys="Escape" />.

<Media
  id="3d-props-polygon"
  alt="Polygon is chosen in the Props panel, then three clicks near the back sideline set three corners and a double-click sets the fourth and finishes a four-sided prop."
/>

Corners and ends snap to the coordinate rounding grid, as dragged marchers do ([Snap to a grid of steps](/guides/marchers/move-marchers/#snap-to-a-grid-of-steps)). Freehand outlines don't snap.

A new prop is on the field from the playhead's count, not before. It starts as a <Ui k="props.surface.floor" plain /> prop with no height, like a tarp; give it a height (below) to make it a riser or a wall.

On a tablet, tap the corners of an arc or a polygon. The note's <Ui k="props.draw.undoPoint" /> takes the last one back, and for a polygon <Ui k="props.draw.finish" /> ends it once it has three corners.

## Change a prop's name, color and size

<Steps>

1. Right-click the prop on the field and choose the edit item, which carries the prop's name, such as <Ui k="actions.items.editNamed" values={{ name: "Riser" }} />. Or select <Ui k="props.title" /> in the sidebar, then <Ui k="props.edit" /> (the pencil) next to the prop.

2. Change what you want in the <Ui k="props.editTitle" plain /> panel.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-props-edit"
  alt="The Edit Prop panel: Name Riser, Locked off, Color None, Surface Floor, and under Outline from count 20, Width 6 m, Depth 2 m, Height 1.2 m, Rotation 0°, Transition 1 counts, and Roll-out set to Doesn't roll out."
  maxWidth={480}
/>

- <Ui k="props.name" />: shown on the field next to the prop when names are shown. Without a name, the prop goes by its number.
- <Ui k="props.lockLabel" />: on, the prop has one position for the whole show, and dragging it on any page moves it on every page. Off, the default for a prop you draw, it can move from page to page (below).
- <Ui k="props.color" />: colors the prop on the field and in the 3D View. <Ui k="props.colorNone" plain /> goes back to the plain look. To color several props at once, select them on the field: the Props panel shows one color for all of them.
- <Ui k="props.surfaceLabel" />: <Ui k="props.surface.floor" plain />, <Ui k="props.surface.obstacle" plain /> or <Ui k="props.surface.platform" plain />. In this version the surface doesn't change how the prop is drawn, and marchers don't stand on platforms.
- <Ui k="props.width" />, <Ui k="props.depth" /> and <Ui k="props.height" />, in meters, and <Ui k="props.rotation" /> in degrees clockwise.
- <Ui k="props.rollOut.label" /> (floor props only): which edge a tarp rolls out from. It's saved with the prop, but in this version it doesn't change how the prop is drawn.

### Grow, shrink or turn a prop during the show

The size and turn are set at a count. The heading <Ui k="props.outlineFrom" values={{ count: "N" }} plain /> names the count the playhead is on, and a change you make goes on that count. The prop keeps its earlier size until then; if it already has a change on that count, yours replaces it.

<Steps>

1. With the prop's <Ui k="props.editTitle" plain /> panel open, move the playhead to the count where the change should be complete.

2. Change <Ui k="props.width" plain />, <Ui k="props.depth" plain />, <Ui k="props.height" plain /> or <Ui k="props.rotation" plain />.

3. Set <Ui k="props.transition" /> to the number of counts the change takes. With 8, the prop turns or resizes over the 8 counts up to that count; with 1, it changes on the count.

</Steps>

Before the prop's first count, the panel says the prop is off the field, and you can't change its size there.

## Resize and turn a prop on the field

Click inside a prop's outline to select it. Drag a side or a corner handle to resize it, or the round handle to turn it, about its center. Like the panel, a resize or turn is saved at the playhead's count. Dragging the handles never moves the prop.

## Move a prop during the show

Select the prop and drag it on a later page, or use the alignment tools, as you would a marcher ([Move marchers](/guides/marchers/move-marchers/)). It travels there during that page's <Term id="move">move</Term>, and it's held in place when nothing moves it. Props aren't in the marcher list.

## Hide or delete a prop

The Props panel lists every prop:

- The eye next to a prop hides it on the field and in the 3D View, or shows it again. Hiding is a view setting on your computer; it doesn't change the show.
- <Ui k="props.showNames" /> shows or hides every prop's name on the field.
- Select a prop's row to select the prop on the field.
- <Ui k="props.delete" /> (the trash can) deletes the prop. Undo (<Kbd action="performUndo" />) brings it back.

## Props in the 3D View

In the 3D View each prop is a solid block: its outline raised to its height, in its color or plain gray. A prop with no height is a thin slab on the field. Blocks move, turn and resize with the timeline. They have no pictures or textures. Props aren't in exports: not on coordinate sheets, drill charts or videos, and not sent to On the Move.

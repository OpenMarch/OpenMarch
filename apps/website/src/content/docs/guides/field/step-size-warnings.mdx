<InShort>Open settings (<Kbd action="openSettings" />), choose <Ui k="settings.field" /> and turn on <Ui k="settings.field.stepSizeWarnings" />: moves with steps too large to march draw red. Set the limit in the Field panel's <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.stepSizeWarningThreshold" />.</InShort>

A step size warning means a marcher has to take steps longer than your field allows to get through a <Term id="move">move</Term> on time. It's a warning, not an error: the show still plays and exports. Use it to find moves you'll want to give more counts or a shorter path.

## Turn on the red paths

<Ui k="settings.field.stepSizeWarnings" /> is off until you turn it on, and applies to every show on this computer. The inspector and the shape tools warn whether it's on or off.

## What a warning looks like

<Media
  id="field-step-warnings"
  alt="Guards G1 to G8 and G11 to G21 on the field with their paths for the move to the next page: G3's and G5's paths are red, dashed and thicker because their steps are over the limit, while every other path is an ordinary thin green line."
/>

- On the field, the marcher's path for the move is drawn red, dashed and thicker. It shows even when paths are turned off (<Kbd action="toggleNextPagePaths" />), so a long step can't hide.
- With start flag positions showing, any stretch of a drawn path that's over the limit is red and dashed.
- In the inspector, a step size over the limit has a red warning icon (<Ui k="inspector.marcher.stepSizeWarning" />). With several marchers selected, <Ui k="inspector.marcher.maxStepSize" /> names the one with the longest step; select the drill number to select that marcher.
- When you place marchers in a shape, marchers who would need too long a step are drawn in orange, and the shape's notes say how many.

## Set the largest step allowed

<Steps>

1. Select <Ui k="sidebar.field" /> in the sidebar.
2. On the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.setup" /> tab, enter the longest step in <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.stepSizeWarningThreshold" />, in inches or centimeters.
3. Select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.done" />.

</Steps>

Under the box, the panel shows the limit the way drill writers say it: 45 inches is "4 to 5", and 22.5 inches is "<Term id="8-to-5">8 to 5</Term>". A step counts as too long when it's more than half an inch over the limit. The default is 45 inches, and 0 turns the warnings off for this show.

The limit is saved with this show's field, so another show keeps its own unless you copy the field to it (see [Edit the field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/#use-this-field-in-another-show)). Only the switch for the red paths is the same for every show.

This tutorial takes you from an empty file to a short stretch of drill you can play back: an opening set, two more sets, and the marchers walking between them. It uses Basic mode, where you work page by page the way a drill chart does. If you chose Expert when OpenMarch first opened, [switch to Basic](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/#switch-between-basic-and-expert) for this tutorial: Play works differently in Expert.

You'll need OpenMarch installed ([Install OpenMarch](/guides/start/install/)). Music is optional: an audio file of the show, a MusicXML score, or a tempo.

## 1. Make the show

1. On the start page, select <Ui k="launchpage.files.createNew" />.
2. Choose <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.start.blank" />, then select <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.next" />.
3. Enter a <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.showName" plain /> and choose a folder to save it in. The show is one `.dots` file, and OpenMarch saves it for you as you work.
4. On <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.ensemble.title" plain />, choose your activity.
5. On <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.field.title" plain />, choose a field template that matches where you perform, such as a high school football field.
6. On <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.performers.title" plain />, choose a section and enter how many marchers it has.
7. Select the create button, which names what it makes (for example **Create 8 Trumpet Marchers**). Each marcher gets a drill number from the section's prefix, such as T1 to T8 for eight trumpets. Repeat this step and the one before it for each section.

   <Media
     id="start-new-show-performers"
     alt="The new-show window on the Performers step, with Quantity 8 and Section Trumpet entered, and the button reading Create 8 Trumpet Marchers."
     maxWidth={560}
   />

8. On <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.frontEnsemble.title" plain />, leave <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.none" plain /> chosen, or choose a size to start with a pit in front of the field. [Set up the front ensemble](/guides/3d-view/front-ensemble/) explains what each size adds.
9. On <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.audio.title" plain />, choose your show's audio file, or select <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.skip" />.
10. On <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.tempo.title" plain />, choose <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.tempo.tempoOnly" plain /> and enter the tempo and time signature, or choose <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.tempo.musicXml" plain /> to read the counts from a score.
11. Select <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.create" />.

The show opens with your marchers standing in rows near a corner of the field.

  id="start-new-show"

  caption="The field in the middle, the toolbar across the top, the sidebar on the left and the timeline at the bottom."
/>

[The window](/guides/workspace/the-window/) names each part. You can add, rename and remove marchers later in the <Ui k="sidebar.marchers" /> panel ([Add, edit and remove marchers](/guides/marchers/add-marchers/)), and change the music in the timeline ([Add the show's music](/guides/music/audio/)).

## 2. Set the opening set

The start of the show is called home: it's where every marcher stands before the first move. While you're there, the editing line over the field says "Editing home positions".

1. Make sure the playhead, the line on the timeline, is at the very start. Press <Kbd action="firstPage" /> if it isn't.
2. Select a section: drag a box around its marchers on the field, or choose the section in the <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" /> tab.
3. Put them in a block. Press <Kbd keys="B" />, drag on the field where the block should go, and select <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" />. The marchers walk to the box's spots.

<Media
  id="start-first-show-box"
  alt="At home, with the eight trumpets T1 to T8 selected in their row at the back corner, B is pressed and a drag below them draws a 4 × 2 box of spots. The pointer selects Place, and the trumpets move onto the spots."
/>

Do the same for the other sections, or drag marchers one by one. [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/) covers lines, arcs and spacing.

## 3. Go to Page 1

A page is a stretch of counts that ends at a set, like a page of a drill chart. Each page is a box on the timeline, and its right end, the page's flag, is the count where marchers arrive at the set. With <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.tempo.tempoOnly" plain />, a new show starts with five pages of four measures each.

Select Page 1's box on the timeline. The page is selected, and the playhead goes to its end.

<Media
  id="start-select-first-page"
  alt="In a new show, the pointer selects the box of Page 1 on the timeline. The box is outlined and the playhead moves to its end."
/>

The marchers haven't moved yet: on a new page they stand where the last set left them, until you move them.

## 4. Write the set

With Page 1 selected, whatever you change on the field is where the marchers will be at the end of Page 1.

1. Select a section and drag it to its new place. Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" /> while dragging for fine control.
2. Or draw a new shape for them, as in the opening set.
3. Move the other sections.

<Media
  id="start-first-show-move"
  alt="With Page 1 selected, the eight trumpets in their block are dragged toward the 50. Then, with Shift held, the block is dragged on and moves more slowly than the pointer."
/>

You set only where marchers end up. OpenMarch walks them from the opening set to Page 1's set over the page's counts, in a straight line at an even step size. Steps too long to march can show as red paths, once you turn that on ([Step size warnings](/guides/field/step-size-warnings/)); it starts off.

## 5. Play it back

Press <Kbd action="playPause" /> to play. In Basic, play starts at the start of the page you're on; select the home icon at the start of the timeline first to play from the top. Press it again to stop.

You can also drag the playhead along the timeline to walk through the move count by count.

<Media
  id="start-first-show-play"
  alt="In Basic, the playhead is dragged from home across Page 1, count by count, and the trumpets walk from their opening block to Page 1's set. The editing line names each count of page 1 as it passes, and at the page's end reads Editing page 1."
/>

## 6. Keep going

Select Page 2's box and set it, then the next. To add a page after the last one, select a count past its end on the timeline, then the button that appears over the shaded counts, such as <Ui k="timeline.appendAtPlayhead.label" values={{ counts: 8 }} /> ([Add and change pages](/guides/timeline/pages/)). A few things that help along the way:

- Select any page's box to go back and change its set. Marchers you haven't moved on a later page follow along.
- Drag a page's flag to give the page more counts and the next one fewer.
- Undo anything with <Kbd action="performUndo" />.
- Select <Ui k="view3d.open" /> in the toolbar to watch the show from the stands.

When you have a few sets, print coordinate sheets or drill charts for your marchers: select <Ui k="exportCoordinates.exportButton" /> in the <Ui k="inspector.shape.fileTab" plain /> tab.

Paste mirrored copies a formation to the other side of a line you choose.

**What to try**

- Select some marchers on side 1 and copy them (Ctrl/Cmd+C).
- Select the same number of marchers on side 2, right-click the field and
  choose **Paste mirrored** (Ctrl+Alt+V, or ⌥⌘V on a Mac). The mirror
  image appears across the 50, with a pink point and line.
- Drag the pink mirror point onto another yard line, a hash or a marcher.
  The paste follows, and the line's name shows at the top of the field.
- Press the arrow keys: the mirror point moves a step at a time. Click a
  spot on the field away from the paste: the point jumps there.
- Drag the paste itself. The pink point follows halfway between.
- In the floating editor, pick **Front to back** under **Mirror**: the
  paste mirrors across the level line through the point. Pick **Both** and
  it turns half a turn around the point.
- Press Enter to place it. Paste mirrored again: it starts at the point
  you left it.
- The paste only mirrors: there's no knob or handles, Alt+R doesn't turn
  it, and dragging anywhere on it moves it.
- Without pasting: select some marchers, pick a corner in the Arrange
  section's pivot grid, and press Alt+F. The group flips across that line.

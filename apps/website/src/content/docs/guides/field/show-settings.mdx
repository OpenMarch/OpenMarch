Show settings are saved in the show file, so they travel with it. Settings that apply to every show on your computer, such as the theme and shortcuts, are in [Settings](/guides/reference/settings/).

## Cancel or reset

<Ui k="workspaceSettings.cancel" /> closes the panel without saving. <Ui k="workspaceSettings.resetToDefaults" /> puts every setting but the show's name, designer and client back to its default straight away, without waiting for Save. Except for <Ui k="workspaceSettings.audioOffsetSeconds" />, which is one step to undo, show settings aren't part of undo, so Undo doesn't bring them back.

## The show's name and credits

<Ui k="workspaceSettings.showInfo.projectName" />, <Ui k="workspaceSettings.showInfo.designer" /> and <Ui k="workspaceSettings.showInfo.client" /> are the show's name, who designed the drill and who it's for. All three are optional. The video boards in the 3D View show them ([Change the venue and uniforms](/guides/3d-view/venue-and-uniforms/#put-the-shows-name-on-the-video-boards)); without a show name, they show the file's name.

## Numbers and timing

- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.pageNumberOffset" />: the first page's number. The default, 0, numbers pages 0, 1, 2 and so on; enter 1 to start at page 1. Every later page, subsets included, counts on from it.
- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.measurementOffset" />: the first <Term id="measure">measure</Term>'s number. The default is 1; enter 0 to call a pickup measure 0. To renumber from a measure partway through, see [Set the tempo to match the music](/guides/music/tempo/#renumber-measures).
- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.audioOffsetSeconds" />: how many seconds after count 1 the music starts, the same number as the music offset in Edit music. See [Add the show's music](/guides/music/audio/#set-where-the-music-starts).
- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.defaultNewPageCounts" />: how long each page is when an export adds pages to the end of the show for moves that run past it (<Ui k="exportCoordinates.addPagesToEnd" />). The add-page button on the timeline doesn't use it: its page runs from the last page to the playhead.
- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.defaultTempo" /> and <Ui k="workspaceSettings.defaultBeatsPerMeasure" />: the tempo and time signature chosen when the show was created. Changing them doesn't change the show's counts; set tempos and time signatures in Edit music ([Set the tempo to match the music](/guides/music/tempo/)).

## How marchers move in the 3D View

<Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.title" /> sets how the marchers march in the 3D View, everywhere in the show:

- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.stepOffFoot.label" />: the foot every move steps off with, <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.stepOffFoot.left" /> by default.
- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.dotMode.label" />: with <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.dotMode.foot" /> (the default), the foot that lands on the last count is on the dot and the marcher centers on it as the feet close; with <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.dotMode.body" />, the middle of the body is over the dot.
- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.directionChange.label" />: how a wind player's moving leg gets to a new direction. <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.directionChange.corner" /> (the default) is the handbook's placement and cut at 8 to 5; other step sizes still blend. <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.directionChange.direct" /> is a plain blend.
- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.holdWhileBandMoves.label" />: what every marcher standing still does while others move, whether it's Held or Not defined. <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.holdWhileBandMoves.stand" /> by default. With <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.holdWhileBandMoves.markTime" /> or <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.holdWhileBandMoves.highMarkTime" />, they mark time on the counts where anyone in the band is moving, and stand while the whole band is still. A <Term id="hold">hold</Term> with its own <Term id="hold-style">hold style</Term> ignores this; to set one hold's style, see [Make marchers stand still](/guides/timeline/holds/#stand-or-mark-time-on-a-hold).
- <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.percussionTravel.label" />: <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.percussionTravel.crab" /> (the default) keeps drummers' hips square to the front; <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.percussionTravel.slide" /> moves them like the winds.

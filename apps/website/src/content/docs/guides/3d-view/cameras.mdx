<InShort>In the 3D View, drag to orbit, drag with the right mouse button to pan, and scroll to zoom. Press <Kbd keys="1" /> to <Kbd keys="9" /> to jump to a camera.</InShort>

You can look at the show from anywhere: move the view with the mouse, jump to one of the venue's cameras, or sit in a seat in the stands. Cameras you add or change are saved with the show.

## Move the view

These are the default controls, called <Ui k="settings.view3d.controls.orbit" plain />:

| Do this                                                                       | To                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Drag                                                                          | Orbit around the field                                |
| Drag with the right mouse button, or hold <Kbd keys="Shift" /> while dragging | Pan across the field                                  |
| Scroll or pinch                                                               | Zoom toward the pointer                               |
| Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" /> or <Kbd keys="Alt" /> while scrolling               | Pan                                                   |
| Double-click a spot on the field or a seat in the stands                      | Orbit around that spot from now on                    |

When you pan, the spot you pressed stays under the pointer, whether it's the field, a performer or the seats. When that spot is far away or seen at a low angle, the view pans at a steady speed instead, so it doesn't jump. Zooming heads toward whatever is under the pointer.

The camera never goes below the ground. As soon as you move the view, the camera menu reads <Ui k="view3d.overlay.freeCamera" plain />; choose a camera to go back to a set position.

To drag to pan instead, open <Ui k="view3d.settings.panelTitle" /> (the gear) in the 3D View, go to the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.preferences" plain /> tab and choose <Ui k="settings.view3d.controls.map" plain />, or <Ui k="settings.view3d.controls.trackpad" plain /> if you use a trackpad. [Settings](/guides/reference/settings/#3d-view) says what each one does. The choice is kept on this computer, not in the show.

On a touch screen, one finger orbits and two fingers pan and zoom, whichever controls you choose ([Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/)).

## Go to a camera

Every venue comes with its own cameras, such as the press box, the front row and a view from straight above.

<Steps>

1. In the 3D View, select the camera menu at the top left. It shows the name of the camera you're on.

2. Choose a camera. The view flies there. The menu lists the same cameras as the panel pictured under [Save your own cameras](#save-your-own-cameras).

</Steps>

Or press <Kbd keys="1" /> to <Kbd keys="9" /> for the first nine cameras in the menu. Choosing the camera you're already on brings the view back to it after you've moved away.

## Sit in the stands

In a venue with stands, you can watch from any seat.

<Steps>

1. Select <Ui k="view3d.overlay.pickSeat" /> (the armchair next to the camera menu).

2. Click a seat in the stands. The view flies there and looks at the field from that seat's height.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-pick-seat"
  alt="Pick a seat is selected next to the camera menu, the pointer moves to a seat near the top of the visitors' stands across the field, and a click flies the view there, looking over the field at the band and the home stands."
/>

Press <Kbd keys="Escape" /> to stop picking without moving. Spectators around your seat step aside so they don't block the view.

## Follow a performer

Watch one performer through the show, from behind them or through their own eyes.

<Steps>

1. Select <Ui k="view3d.overlay.followPerformer" /> (the walking figure next to the camera menu), or press <Kbd keys="P" />.

2. Click a performer on the field. The view flies behind them and the camera menu shows who you follow, such as <Ui k="view3d.overlay.following" values={{ name: "T1" }} plain />. As they march, the camera moves with them, and when they turn it swings round to stay behind them.

3. To see what they see, select <Ui k="view3d.overlay.pointOfView" /> (the eye), or press <Kbd keys="V" />. The view moves into their eyes and turns as they turn. Their arms and instrument are in front of you, as they'd see them. Their own head and hat are left out, so nothing blocks the view when you look up.

</Steps>

While you follow someone from behind, drag to orbit around them and scroll to zoom in or out; they stay in the middle of the view, and the camera keeps the angle you chose as they turn. Through their eyes, drag to look around and scroll to zoom.

Two switches in <Ui k="view3d.settings.panelTitle" /> (the gear), on the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.preferences" plain /> tab, change this, on this computer only:

- <Ui k="view3d.settings.followTurns" plain />: off, the camera behind them keeps facing the same way on the field when they turn.
- <Ui k="view3d.settings.povShowsBody" plain />: off, their own body and instrument are hidden, for a clear view.

To stop, select <Ui k="view3d.overlay.stopFollowing" /> (the cross) or press <Kbd keys="Escape" />: from their eyes, the first <Kbd keys="Escape" /> goes back behind them and the second stops following. Panning, double-clicking, sitting in a seat or choosing a camera also stops following.

## Save your own cameras

The cameras are a list you can change. It's saved in the show, one list for each venue, and the 3D View opens on the first camera in it. Changes are saved straight away, and undo in the editor doesn't undo them.

<Steps>

1. Select the camera menu, then <Ui k="view3d.cameras.edit" />. The <Ui k="view3d.cameras.title" plain /> panel opens under the menu.

2. Move the view to where you want a camera.

3. Select <Ui k="view3d.cameras.addView" />. A new camera is added at the end of the list, with its name ready to type.

4. Type a name and press <Kbd keys="Enter" />.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-cameras-panel"
  alt="The Cameras panel under the camera menu: six cameras, each row with its number, name and buttons to set it to the current view, move it up or down, or remove it; the note that the number is the camera's key and the view opens on camera 1; and Add current view and Reset to defaults at the bottom."
  maxWidth={480}
/>

Each row starts with the camera's number key: select it to fly there. Then comes the name, which you can change in place, and these buttons:

- <Ui k="view3d.cameras.setView" />: moves the camera to where the view is now.
- <Ui k="view3d.cameras.moveUp" /> and <Ui k="view3d.cameras.moveDown" />: change the order, and with it the number keys. The camera at the top is the one the 3D View opens on.
- <Ui k="view3d.cameras.remove" />: removes the camera. Select <Ui k="view3d.cameras.undo" /> in the panel within a few seconds to bring it back.

A venue can have up to 30 cameras. <Ui k="view3d.cameras.reset" /> replaces your list with the venue's own cameras; if you've changed the list, it asks first, because your list isn't kept anywhere else.

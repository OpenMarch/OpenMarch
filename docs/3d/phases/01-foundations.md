### P1.1: World mapping in core

- Owner: claude-p1.1 (3d/p1-world)
- Status: done
- PR: https://github.com/AlexDumo/OpenMarch-timeline/pull/57
- Parallel: yes
- Depends on: —

Add `packages/core/src/field/world.ts` and export it from `@openmarch/core`: `stepMeters`, `pixelsToWorld`, `worldToPixels`, `stepsToWorld` and `fieldFootprint` (design §2).

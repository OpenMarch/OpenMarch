<InShort>In the <Ui k="sidebar.marchers" /> panel, select <Ui k="frontEnsemble.summary.setUp" /> next to <Ui k="frontEnsemble.summary.title" plain />, choose a size and select <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.addPit" />.</InShort>

The front ensemble, or pit, is the equipment that stands in front of the field all show: marimbas, vibraphones, synths, racks, a drum set, speakers, and the drum major's podium. You set it up once and it stays put on every page. On the field each piece is drawn from above; in the 3D View it's the real instrument, with its player standing at it.

A new show can start with a pit: its <Ui k="launchpage.newShow.steps.frontEnsemble.title" plain /> step offers the same sizes as the presets below ([Write your first show](/guides/start/first-show/)).

## Open the front ensemble

<Steps>

1. Select <Ui k="sidebar.marchers" /> in the sidebar.

2. At the top of the panel, the <Ui k="frontEnsemble.summary.title" plain /> row says what the show has. Select <Ui k="frontEnsemble.summary.setUp" />, or <Ui k="frontEnsemble.summary.edit" /> once there's a pit.

</Steps>

The front ensemble view lists the show's equipment and its podiums. The command palette's <Ui k="actions.ui.openFrontEnsemble" /> opens it too.

## Start from a preset

<Steps>

1. While the show has no pit, the front ensemble view shows the presets: <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.small" plain />, <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.medium" plain /> and <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.large" plain />, each pictured from above with what it holds. Choose one.

2. Leave <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.addPerformers" plain /> on to get a player for each instrument and a drum major on the podium, then select <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.addPit" />.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-front-ensemble-presets"
  alt="The front ensemble view of a show with no pit yet: Small, Medium and Large as cards, each with a picture of the pit from above and what it holds, Medium chosen, Also add performers on, and Add pit."
  maxWidth={480}
/>

The pit is laid out in two rows in front of the front sideline, centered on the 50, with the speakers on the yard lines either side, a podium on the 50 and a mixer behind the pit. Small and Medium use 4.3-octave marimbas, Large uses 4.6, and the vibraphones are 3.0 octaves. Applying is one undo step.

<Media
  id="3d-front-ensemble-field"
  alt="A Medium pit on the field below the front sideline, drawn from above on a light gray area: a rack, xylophone, drum set, glockenspiel and synth in the back row, vibraphones and marimbas in the front row, each with its player's dot on the field side of it, speakers at the ends, the drum major on the podium in front, and the mixer behind the pit."
/>

Once the show has a pit, the presets move behind <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.reset" /> in the view's header: choose a size there and select <Ui k="frontEnsemble.presets.apply" />. Applying another size replaces the equipment the last preset placed; equipment you added yourself stays. The last preset's players move onto the new instruments of their section, and those whose instrument the new size doesn't have are removed, unless they have moves later in the show. Undo brings everything back.

## Add one piece

<Steps>

1. In the front ensemble view, select <Ui k="frontEnsemble.add.button" />.

2. Find the piece: the pictures are grouped under <Ui k="frontEnsemble.groups.keyboards" plain />, <Ui k="frontEnsemble.groups.percussion" plain />, <Ui k="frontEnsemble.groups.audio" plain /> and <Ui k="frontEnsemble.groups.podiums" plain />, or type in <Ui k="frontEnsemble.search" plain /> ("marimba black", "tower"). Each shows its size and footprint in meters; a dot marks the usual choice.

3. Select the picture. The panel closes, and the piece follows the pointer.

4. Click the field where it goes, usually below the front sideline. A podium placed behind the field turns to face the band.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-front-ensemble-add"
  alt="The Add equipment view: a search box, then under Keyboards a grid of pictures of marimbas, vibraphones, xylophones, a glockenspiel and synths, each named with its size, finish and footprint."
  maxWidth={480}
/>

A piece you add this way has no player. To give it one, add a marcher and put their dot on the field side of the instrument, about a foot behind its edge, where a preset puts its players. The command palette's <Ui k="actions.ui.addFrontEnsembleEquipment" /> opens the same pictures.

## Change, move or remove a piece

Equipment is <Ui k="props.locked" plain />: it has one position for the whole show. Dragging it on any page moves it on every page, and a selection box around marchers doesn't pick it up.

To change a piece, right-click it on the field and choose <Ui k="actions.items.editNamed" values={{ name: "Marimba 1" }} />, or select <Ui k="frontEnsemble.equipment.edit" plain /> on its row in the front ensemble view. There you can rename it, choose another size or finish of the same instrument, or turn <Ui k="props.locked" plain /> off to move it during the show the way you move a prop ([Add props to the field](/guides/3d-view/props/)).

To remove a piece, select <Ui k="frontEnsemble.equipment.delete" plain /> on its row. Undo brings it back.

## How the pit looks

On the field, each piece is its outline from above; zoomed in, the bars, drum heads, steps and a mark on the side it faces fill in. A plain tone marks the area the pit stands on, only as wide and deep as the equipment.

In the 3D View each piece is the real instrument at its real size. A player whose dot is at an instrument faces the audience and stands at it, and the drum major stands on the podium, facing the band. The players stand still: there's no playing animation in this version.

OpenMarch 0.2 changes how drill is written: pages now sit on a timeline, marchers can move on their own schedule within and across pages, and the field has proper shape tools. Shows from 0.1.7 open in 0.2 after a one-time conversion; [Coming from OpenMarch 0.1](/guides/start/coming-from-0-1/) explains what changes.

## The timeline

Pages, moves, counts, measures and the music sit on one timeline under the field. You set where marchers are at a count, and OpenMarch walks them there. Each marcher on each page is Defined, Held or Not defined, so a later page follows your changes to an earlier one unless you've decided otherwise.

In Expert, a move can start and end on any count, run across pages, cover only some marchers and overlap other moves. Basic keeps the page-by-page way of working, with positions in the middle of a page when you need them.

See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/), [Moves](/guides/timeline/moves/) and [Defined, Held, Not defined](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/).

## Basic and Expert

Two editor modes: Basic works in pages, like a drill chart, and Expert shows the full timeline. You choose when you first open 0.2 and can switch at any time. See [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

## Shapes

Draw a box, circle, line, curve or arc on the field and place the selected marchers in it. Spots snap to half steps, yard lines and hashes, and a placed shape can be edited afterward. See [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

## Edit music

Line the counts up with the music in the timeline: drag the tempo until the counts sit on the beats, tap a tempo, and set tempo changes, holds and time signatures. MusicXML import plays out repeats and shows what it found. See [Line the counts up with the music](/guides/music/tempo/) and [Import a MusicXML score](/guides/music/musicxml/).

## The 3D View

Watch the show from the stands in a window of its own, with marchers in uniform, in a stadium you choose, kept in step with the editor. See [Open the 3D View](/guides/3d-view/open-the-3d-view/).

## Props

Draw props on the field, size and turn them, and move them on the timeline like marchers. In the 3D View they show as plain blocks. See [Props](/guides/3d-view/props/).

## Seeing and playing the show

- Preview, a watch-only view of the show: [Watch the show in Preview](/guides/workspace/editor-and-preview/).
- Start flag positions and paths, which show where marchers come from and go to: [See where marchers go](/guides/timeline/see-where-marchers-go/).
- Playback speed, loop and follow playhead: [Play the show](/guides/timeline/play-the-show/).

## Working faster

- The command palette finds any command by name (<Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />).
- Shortcuts can be changed in Settings, and every one is listed in [Keyboard shortcuts](/guides/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Beyond the desktop

- The web editor runs OpenMarch in a browser, and keeps your shows in the browser's own storage.
- On a tablet, the window switches to a layout made for touch and a pen.
- Video export makes a video of the show from the field.

See [Install OpenMarch](/guides/start/install/) and [Export a video](/guides/files/export-video/).

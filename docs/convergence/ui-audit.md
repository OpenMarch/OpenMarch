## Top 20 cuts

1. **Show checks inspector section** (Inspector.tsx:38): it is the props/cues/notes feature leaking in, and shows "Nothing to check" in every state.
2. **"Visible sections" and "Field direction"** (FieldDisplayBar.tsx:75-86): "coming soon" buttons that do nothing.
3. **Perspective button and its commented-out code** (CanvasZoomControls.tsx:187-208): always disabled.
4. **Run field benchmark** in ⌘K (definitions.ts:645): a developer diagnostic.
5. **3D readout "Eye … up · … to center"** (Readout.tsx:208-213): debug camera numbers.
6. **3D "Preview only" section** (Horn state, Guard equipment, Step off) (SettingsPanel.tsx:107-115,397): test controls tied to cues.
7. **Collisions "Zoom to review", the overlay and `showCollisions`** (CollisionsTab.tsx:70, UiSettingsStore.ts:287): they do nothing because the setting is forced off. Fix them or hide the tab.
8. **Plugins settings section** (sections.ts:182): the plugin API is gone (#255).
9. **Remove keyframe and the "…later pages move back too" variants** (UnsetHereButton.tsx, definitions.ts:445,453,911,923, FieldContextMenu.tsx:74, TimelineKeyframes.tsx:245): fold them into one "Clear" action with one name.
10. **"Place selected marchers ▾" in the inspector** (MarcherEditor.tsx:409-458): the same items are in the toolbar, the field menu and the timeline page menu.
11. **The Transform section's duplicate Flip pair**, and hide Transform for a single marcher (TransformSection.tsx:30,98-136 vs MarcherEditor.tsx:302-321).
12. **Move card Name, Split at playhead and Delete** (TimelineMoveCard.tsx:244-392): already in the clip menu and on the keys.
13. **Hold line "Pages X–Y show these marchers…" and its "Define as shown" link** (TimelineHoldLine.tsx:136-220): keep only "Last moved on Page X".
14. **Shapes › Box button in the inspector** (ShapeToolSection.tsx:22-41): the 4th entry to Box.
15. **Dead timeline code: "Add selected marchers", "Create Track", and the Mute/Metronome buttons** (TimelineRangeMenu.tsx:628, TimelineVariants.tsx:567, TimelineControls.tsx:28-110).
16. **3D Settings "Pause drawing" and "Save power on battery"**: drop them, and fold Resolution into Quality (View3dSettings.tsx:64-102). Also keep Quality and Resolution in only one place (SettingsPanel.tsx:352,379).
17. **"While shaping" fade setting** (FieldSettings.tsx:103): an A/B leftover, so pick one.
18. **One way to add a page**: keep either the "+ Add N-Count Page" pill or the playhead menu item (TimelineAppendAtPlayhead.tsx vs AddPageFlagMenu.tsx).
19. **Camera profiles and "Save current view"** (CamerasPanel.tsx:123-216,368-689; CameraBar.tsx:112): the profiles are tied to the Library; Save current view duplicates "Add current view".
20. **Duplicate chrome**: the File-tab Undo/Redo (FileTab.tsx:62-79), the Compact button vs the splitter (TimelineControls.tsx:317), the `/` command-bar key (definitions.ts:704), the second "Open 3D View" setting (GeneralSettings.tsx:132 vs AutoOpenQuestion.tsx), and Help › Blender starter kit (application-menu.ts:215).

4. **Move card's Delete / Split at playhead / Name** (TimelineMoveCard.tsx:244-392). Each one is in the clip right-click menu, and Delete is also on the Delete key. The card could shrink to the counts and Select them.

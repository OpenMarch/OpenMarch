<InShort>In the 3D View, select <Ui k="view3d.settings.panelTitle" /> (the gear): choose a <Ui k="view3d.overlay.venue" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.overlay.lighting" plain /> on the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.venue" plain /> tab, and uniforms on the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.performers" plain /> tab.</InShort>

The venue is where the 3D View puts your show: a high school stadium, a college stadium, a domed pro stadium, a gym, an indoor arena, or one of two championship venues modeled on real buildings, with its lighting and crowd. The venue and the uniforms are saved in the show, so everyone who opens it sees the same thing.

## Change the venue

<Steps>

1. In the 3D View, select <Ui k="view3d.settings.panelTitle" /> (the gear at the top right). The <Ui k="view3d.settings.panelTitle" plain /> panel opens on its <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.venue" plain /> tab.

2. On the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.venue" plain /> tab, choose a <Ui k="view3d.overlay.venue" plain />. Under <Ui k="view3d.venueKind.fitted" /> are the venues built around your show's field, whatever its size: <Ui k="view3d.kit.hs" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.bighs" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.college" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.pro" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.gym" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.arena" /> and <Ui k="view3d.kit.blank" />. Under <Ui k="view3d.venueKind.real" /> are the venues modeled on real buildings at their real size: <Ui k="view3d.kit.markDiesel" /> and <Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" />. Point at a real venue to see its size.

3. Choose the <Ui k="view3d.overlay.lighting" plain />. The outdoor stadiums have <Ui k="view3d.lighting.day" />, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.dusk" /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.night" />; the Pro dome has <Ui k="view3d.lighting.day" />, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.night" /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.roofClosed" />; <Ui k="view3d.kit.markDiesel" plain /> has <Ui k="view3d.lighting.roofClosed" />, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.day" /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.night" />; the gym, the arena and <Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" plain /> have <Ui k="view3d.lighting.house" /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.show" /> lights.

</Steps>

Until you choose one, the venue follows the show's field: a football field gets <Ui k="view3d.kit.hs" plain />, an indoor or grid floor gets <Ui k="view3d.kit.gym" plain />, and any other field gets <Ui k="view3d.kit.blank" plain />. Everything on the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.venue" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.performers" plain /> tabs is saved in the show, and each change is a step in the editor's undo history, so <Kbd action="performUndo" /> in the editor takes it back. The <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.preferences" plain /> tab is kept on this computer instead.

<Media
  id="3d-venue-arena"

The field in every venue is your show's field. The stadiums paint it as turf, with its yard lines and hashes. The gym and the arenas print it as a tarp: your field image when the field has one ([Edit the field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/)), or a dark tarp marked every 5 steps. In the stadiums a football field always has both end zones, even when your field stops at the goal lines; the marchers stay where you put them. Other fields, such as an indoor floor, get no end zones. Each venue has its own cameras ([Move the camera](/guides/3d-view/cameras/)). The editor's 2D field can show the venue under its grid too ([See the venue on the field](/guides/3d-view/venue-on-the-field/)).

### Mark Diesel Stadium

<Ui k="view3d.kit.markDiesel" plain /> is a championship stadium with a roof that closes, a full house in the stands, and a field painted from a real stadium's turf plans. It has a football field of its own, whatever your field is. A football field becomes that field. Any other field, such as an indoor floor, lies at midfield as a tarp on the turf: your field image when the field has one, or the dark tarp. The stadium never shrinks around a small floor. Under <Ui k="view3d.lighting.roofClosed" plain /> the roof is shut; <Ui k="view3d.lighting.day" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.night" plain /> open it.

Its midfield has a football helmet with the OpenMarch marcher on it, and its yard numbers are drawn from the stadium's plans.

To see the field exactly as the stadium paints it, choose <Ui k="view3d.settings.fieldLinesOption.stadium" /> under <Ui k="view3d.settings.fieldLines" plain /> on the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.venue" plain /> tab: the lines really painted in the stadium, its own yard numbers and hashes, whatever your field's are. <Ui k="view3d.settings.fieldLinesOption.show" /> paints the lines of your show's field instead, as you set them up with <Ui k="actions.field.editField" />. Marchers stay where you wrote them either way, so on a high school or college field they won't sit on the stadium's hashes.

### Taydon, OH

<Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" plain /> is a championship arena modeled on the one where the winter guard and indoor percussion world championships are held, set up as it is for them: a black tarp floor, a black drape across the back with the performers' tunnel behind it, entry and exit at the back corners, stands all the way around, and a scoreboard hung over the floor.

Its floor doesn't change size. It's 106 by 78 feet, the largest competition area winter guard rules allow, and your field sits at its center. Every indoor and SoundSport field fits; football fields don't. When your field doesn't fit, <Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" plain /> is greyed out in the picker, and selecting it says how big its floor is and how big your field is. If you change the field to a bigger one after choosing <Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" plain />, the 3D View and the venue on the 2D field show the <Ui k="view3d.kit.arena" plain /> instead, and the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.venue" plain /> tab says why. Change the field back and <Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" plain /> returns.

From a seat in <Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" plain />, such as <Ui k="view3d.camera.judges" plain />, everyone whose head would cover part of the floor steps aside too, all the way down to the front row, so you see the whole floor and its front line. The rest of the house stays full, and they come back as soon as you leave the seat.

## Turn the crowd on or off

In a venue with stands, turn <Ui k="view3d.overlay.crowd" /> on or off on the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.venue" plain /> tab, or press <Kbd keys="C" /> in the 3D View. With the crowd on, <Ui k="view3d.settings.crowdColors" plain /> sets the colors the <Ui k="view3d.settings.homeColor" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.settings.awayColor" plain /> sides wear.

## Letter the end zones

Type up to 24 characters in <Ui k="view3d.settings.endZones" /> and choose a color, and both end zones show the lettering. Leave it empty for none. A show that hasn't changed these gets OPENMARCH in purple in <Ui k="view3d.kit.markDiesel" plain />; change it or clear it the same way. The gym, the arena and <Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" plain /> have no end zones.

## Put the show's name on the video boards

The video boards in the <Ui k="view3d.kit.bighs" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.college" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.pro" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.kit.markDiesel" plain /> venues, and the scoreboards in the <Ui k="view3d.kit.arena" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.kit.nightpound" plain />, show the show's name, with its designer and client. Enter them in [Show settings](/guides/field/show-settings/). Without a show name, the boards show the file's name.

## Choose the uniforms

On the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.performers" plain /> tab, <Ui k="view3d.settings.uniforms" /> dresses the band and <Ui k="view3d.settings.guardUniforms" /> dresses the color guard. Choose one of the built-in uniforms for each, such as <Ui k="view3d.uniformPreset.westPoint" /> or <Ui k="view3d.uniformPreset.sash" />. Marchers in guard sections (Color Guard, Flag, Rifle and the other guard sections) wear the guard uniform; everyone else wears the band uniform. You can't design your own uniform in this version.

## Choose the flag silks

<Ui k="view3d.settings.silkShape" /> chooses the shape of the color guard's 6-foot flags: <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.standard" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.curvedTail" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.cSwing" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rainbow" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.arc" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.teardrop" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.distressed" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rounded" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rectangle" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.curvedAngle" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.wings" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.snake" /> or <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.swing" />. Each comes in one common size, such as 36 by 56 inches for the Standard. A show starts with the plain <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rectangle" plain />.

Every guard member's flag gets the same shape for the whole show. <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.curvedTail" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rainbow" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.distressed" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rounded" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.curvedAngle" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.wings" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.snake" plain /> are traced from a chart of common shapes and their sizes are estimates, so check the cut and size with your flag maker before you print. <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.wings" plain /> are worn, but in the 3D View they hang from the pole.

### Paint the silks with your own design

<Steps>

1. Under <Ui k="view3d.settings.silkArtwork" plain />, download the template for the shape you chose with the **PNG**, **SVG** or **PDF** button. It's drawn at the silk's real size, with the pole side on the left. Keep anything important inside the dashed line, the safe area; the grey parts are outside the silk.

2. Paint your design over the template in any image editor and export it as a PNG at the template's size and proportions. Painting past the silk's edge into the bleed is fine; it's cut off.

3. Select <Ui k="view3d.silkArtwork.import" /> and choose the PNG. A picture larger than 4,096 pixels on its longest side is scaled down to that as it comes in, the most detail the 3D View shows. Once it's in, the row shows the picture and its name with <Ui k="view3d.silkArtwork.replace" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.silkArtwork.remove" plain />, as in the picture above.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-silk-artwork"
  alt="The band from the front row: the color guard's Standard-shaped flags show the imported design, a white star and a gold stripe on purple."
/>

The flags show your design, cut to the silk's shape, instead of the guard uniform's pattern or the section's color. The back of the silk shows it reversed, the way a one-sided print looks. If you change the shape afterwards, the same picture stretches to fit, so download that shape's template and paint it again. <Ui k="view3d.silkArtwork.replace" /> chooses another PNG and <Ui k="view3d.silkArtwork.remove" /> takes the artwork off; both are steps you can undo in the editor.

## Choose the horns

<Ui k="view3d.settings.brassFinish" /> chooses how every brass horn looks: <Ui k="view3d.brassFinish.brass" /> (gold) or <Ui k="view3d.brassFinish.silver" />. Woodwinds keep their own: flutes and piccolos stay silver and saxophones gold.

<Ui k="view3d.settings.tuba" /> chooses what the tuba section carries: a <Ui k="view3d.tuba.contra" /> held on the shoulder, or a <Ui k="view3d.tuba.sousaphone" /> worn around the body. Sousaphone players wear a beret instead of the uniform's hat, so the bell has room.

<InShort>Open the Field panel (<Kbd action="fieldEditMode" />) and select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.lines" />. Drag out of a ruler to add a line, drag a line to move it, and press <Kbd action="fieldEditDeleteLines" /> to delete the picked lines.</InShort>

The field's lines are what marchers' coordinates count from. X lines, such as yard lines, say where a marcher is from side to side; Y lines, such as the hashes and sidelines, say where from front to back. A coordinate reads like "2 steps outside the 35, 4 steps behind the front hash".

## Open the Lines tab

<Ui k="actions.field.editLines" /> in the command palette opens the panel straight on this tab. Rulers run along the top and left of the field, the marchers fade, and a note over the field says what to do. Switch to another tab to stop editing lines; select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.done" /> to close the panel. Opening the panel, keeping and throwing away your changes are on [Edit the field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/).

The tab lists the lines one way at a time: choose <Ui k="fieldEditMode.lines.xTab" values={{ count: 21 }} /> or <Ui k="fieldEditMode.lines.yTab" values={{ count: 5 }} />. A crossed-out eye marks a hidden line, and a crosshair a line used as a reference. Under the list, the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.line.title" /> section shows the picked line's values.

## Change both sides at once

<Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.mirror" /> is on when you start: a change to an X line on one side of the 50 makes the same change to its twin on the other side. Turn it off with its button, or press <Kbd action="fieldEditMirror" />, to change one side only.

## Add a line

Drag out of the ruler along the top to add an X line, or out of the ruler down the left to add a Y line, and let go over the field. A note by the pointer says where the line goes and how far it is from the lines on either side. The line moves in whole steps; hold <Kbd keys="Shift" /> for half steps. Let go back over the ruler to add nothing.

You can also select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.line.add" /> next to the list, press <Kbd action="fieldEditAddXLine" /> or <Kbd action="fieldEditAddYLine" />, or double-click a ruler. The line goes where the pointer is, or halfway into the largest gap.

A new line is named from where it is, such as 37.5 yard line, and is picked so you can change it. A spot that already has a line, or a Y line in front of the front sideline, isn't taken; a message says why.

To add many lines at once, choose <Ui k="actions.field.addXCheckpointSeries" /> or <Ui k="actions.field.addYCheckpointSeries" /> in the command palette (<Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />) and enter how many steps apart. They fill the field up to its outermost lines, skipping spots that already have one, and aren't used as references, so coordinates still count from your main lines.

## Pick lines

- Click a line on the field, its tab on the ruler, or its row in the list. On the field, <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> adds a line to the pick or takes it out; in the list, <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="click" /> does that and <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> picks a run of rows.
- Drag a box over the field to pick the lines it crosses. A thin box between the hashes picks the yard lines and no hash.
- Press <Kbd action="fieldEditNextLine" /> and <Kbd action="fieldEditPreviousLine" /> to walk through the lines, and <Kbd keys="Mod+A" /> to pick every line in the list.

## Change a line's position or names

Pick the line and change it in the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.line.title" /> section. A typed value is saved when you press <Kbd keys="Enter" /> or move to another box, and each change is one step to undo. To go straight to the position, double-click the line or press <Kbd action="fieldEditEditLine" />.

- <Ui k="fieldEditMode.popover.position" />: steps from the center (X) or behind the front (Y). Type a number, `+4` or `-4` to move it from where it is, `47.5yd` for a yard line on a football field, or a sum such as `85.33/2`. With <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.mirror" /> off, choose the side under it.
- <Ui k="fieldEditMode.popover.shortName" />: what the labels outside the field and the coordinates show, such as 45 or FH.
- <Ui k="fieldEditMode.popover.name" />: the full name, used in written-out coordinates. Until you choose a venue in the 3D View, the front-to-back lines' names also help it guess one (see [Change the venue and uniforms](/guides/3d-view/venue-and-uniforms/)).
- <Ui k="fieldEditMode.popover.yardNumber" />: the number painted on the field at an X line.

A line keeps the names its position gives it, and is renamed when it moves, until you type your own. Clear a name to go back to the one from its position.

## Hide a line, or stop counting from it

- Turn off <Ui k="fieldEditMode.popover.visible" /> (<Kbd action="fieldEditToggleVisible" />) to stop drawing the line. Coordinates can still count from it, and on this tab it shows as a faint dashed line.
- Turn off <Ui k="fieldEditMode.line.reference" /> (<Kbd action="fieldEditToggleReference" />) for a line you want to see but not count from, such as the real hash next to a grid hash.
- To draw the Y lines as short hash marks, as on a football field, turn on <Ui k="fieldEditMode.list.drawAsHashes" /> above the front-to-back list.

With several lines picked, the section shows both switches (<Ui k="fieldEditMode.line.mixed" /> when only some have it on) and <Ui k="fieldEditMode.line.everyOther" />, which keeps every other line picked.

## Move or delete lines

- Drag a line, or slide its tab along the ruler, to move it. Lines picked on the same axis move together. The arrow keys move picked lines a step, half a step with <Kbd keys="Shift" />. <Kbd keys="Escape" /> during a drag cancels it.
- To delete, drag lines back into their ruler, press <Kbd action="fieldEditDeleteLines" />, or select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.popover.delete" /> in the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.line.title" /> section. A field needs at least two lines each way: where deleting the picked lines would leave fewer, Delete is greyed out and says why, and <Kbd action="fieldEditDeleteLines" /> does nothing.

To delete every 5-yard line on a football field, drag a box from inside one goal line to inside the other, select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.line.everyOther" />, then press <Kbd action="fieldEditDeleteLines" />.

If the outermost X lines end up different distances from the center, a note above the list says so: the field is drawn centered between them.

## Use the keyboard

When the tab opens, the field has the keyboard. <Kbd action="fieldEditNextPane" /> moves between the field and the panel, and <Kbd keys="Escape" /> drops the pick, then closes the panel. The keyboard button at the top of the tab lists the rest; so does the Field group in [Keyboard shortcuts](/guides/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## On a tablet

The rulers and the strip along each line are a finger wide. Tap a picked line to type its position on a number pad, and turn on <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.selectMany" /> at the top of the tab to pick several lines with taps. The rest works as in [Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/).

A step size warning means a marcher has to take steps longer than your field allows to get through a move on time. They're warnings, not errors: the show still plays and exports. Use them to find moves you'll want to give more counts or a shorter path.

The red paths on the field are off until you turn them on. The inspector and the shape tools warn whatever the setting.

## Turn on the red paths

Open settings (<Kbd action="openSettings" />), choose <Ui k="settings.field" />, and turn on <Ui k="settings.field.stepSizeWarnings" />. The setting applies to every show on this computer. Turn it off the same way.

## What a warning looks like

- On the field, the marcher's path for the move is drawn red, dashed and thicker. It shows even when paths are turned off (<Kbd action="toggleNextPagePaths" />), so a long step can't hide.
- With start flag positions showing, any stretch of a drawn path that's over the limit is red and dashed, and the rest stays thin and gray.
- In the inspector, the selected marcher's step size has a red warning icon: <Ui k="inspector.marcher.stepSizeWarning" />. Under <Ui k="inspector.marcher.maxStepSize" />, the drill number of the marcher with the longest step is shown; select the drill number to select that marcher.
- When you place marchers in a shape, marchers who would need too long a step are drawn in orange, and the shape's notes say how many.

## Set the largest step allowed

The limit belongs to the field, so every show on that field uses the same one.

<Steps>

1. Select <Ui k="sidebar.field" /> in the sidebar.
2. If the field is a template, select <Ui k="fieldProperties.customize" />.
3. On the <Ui k="fieldProperties.tabs.general" /> tab, enter the longest step in <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.stepSizeWarningThreshold" />, in inches or centimeters.

</Steps>

The label also shows the limit the way drill writers say it: 45 inches is (4 to 5), four steps every five yards, and 22.5 inches is (8 to 5). A step counts as too long when it's more than half an inch over the limit. The default is 45 inches.

Enter 0 to turn the warnings off for this field, whatever the setting.

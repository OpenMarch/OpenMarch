# Questions a band director might bring to the guide

1. How do I make some marchers stay still for 8 counts while the others move?
2. How do I add a page between page 2 and page 3 without renumbering everything after it?
3. I pressed Play and it started somewhere I didn't expect. Where does Play start from?
4. How do I put 16 marchers in a 4 × 4 block, 2 steps apart?
5. How do I line the counts up with my recording?
6. How do I print drill charts and coordinate sheets?
7. I changed page 3 and marchers moved on later pages too. How do I stop that?
8. On an iPad, how do I select several marchers at once?
9. How do I make some marchers arrive halfway through a page?
10. How do I change the field to college hashes?
11. How do I export a video of the show?
12. How do I bring in my score from MuseScore or Finale?
13. How do I make marchers mark time instead of standing still in the 3D View?
14. I use the web editor. Where is my show saved, and how do I keep a copy?
15. How do I open a show I made in OpenMarch 0.1.7?
16. What is the start flag?
17. How do I turn a group of marchers around one marcher at the corner?
18. How do I turn snapping off so I can drag marchers anywhere?
19. How do I give the trumpets a different color?
20. How do I find moves that have steps too big to march?
21. How do I write a gradual slowdown (a ritardando) into the tempo?
22. How do I select all the trumpets?
23. What's the shortcut to play just the page I'm on?
24. How do I send my show to On the Move?
25. How do I watch the show from the stands in a stadium?
26. How do I put marchers in a circle?
27. How do I cut one move into two?
28. How do I keep replaying a few counts while I work on them?
29. How do I add a riser or a backdrop?
30. How do I set up the front ensemble (the pit)?

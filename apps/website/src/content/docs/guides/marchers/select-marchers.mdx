<InShort>Click a marcher, <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> to add more, or drag a box around them on empty field. Press <Kbd action="selectAllMarchers" /> for everyone.</InShort>

Most tools act on the selected marchers: moving, aligning, placing them in a shape, holding them on a page. The inspector lists the selected marchers' drill numbers.

## Click to select

- Click a marcher to select only that marcher.
- <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> a marcher to add it to the selection, or to take it out if it's already selected.
- Click empty field, or press <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" />, to clear the selection.

## Drag a box

Drag on empty field to draw a box. Every marcher whose dot the box touches is selected, and the previous selection is cleared. While you drag, each marcher the box will take gets a small square, and the number of marchers shows by the pointer.

<Media
  id="marchers-select-box"
  alt="A box is dragged around a row of eight marchers, which become selected. Then a marcher in the row behind them is clicked with Shift held and joins the selection."
/>

- Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="drag" /> as you start the box to add to the selection. The number reads "+3 marchers".
- Hold <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="drag" /> as you start the box to take marchers out. The squares are dashed, and the number reads "−3 marchers".

A drag that starts inside the frame around several selected marchers moves them instead. Start the box outside the frame, or clear the selection first.

## Draw a lasso

A lasso selects a group that isn't a rectangle, such as a curved line. The small bar at the field's top right picks what a drag on empty field draws: <Ui k="fieldSelectTool.box" /> or <Ui k="fieldSelectTool.lasso" />.

<Media
  id="marchers-select-tool"
  alt="The bar at the field's top right with two buttons, Box select and Lasso select, and Lasso select picked. Its tooltip reads Lasso select with the V key: draw around marchers, or double-click and drag with Box for a lasso too."
/>

1. Pick <Ui k="fieldSelectTool.lasso" /> in that bar, or press <Kbd action="toggleLassoSelect" />.
2. Drag around the marchers you want.
3. Bring the pointer back near where you started, until the line turns green, and let go.

<Media
  id="marchers-select-lasso"
  alt="With Lasso select picked, a drag on empty field draws a freehand line around a ring of marchers. The line closes back at its start, and every marcher inside it is selected."
/>

Every marcher whose dot is inside the lasso is selected. If you let go far from the start, it selects nothing. <Kbd keys="Shift" /> and <Kbd keys="Alt" /> add and take away, as they do for a box.

The tool you pick stays picked on this device until you change it. To draw one lasso while <Ui k="fieldSelectTool.box" /> is picked, double-click empty field, hold the button down on the second click, and drag.

## Select everyone

Press <Kbd action="selectAllMarchers" />, or right-click empty field and choose <Ui k="actions.select.selectAll" />. Props are included, but not [locked](/guides/3d-view/props/#lock-a-prop) ones such as the pit's equipment.

## Select a whole section, tag, family or drill prefix

The toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" /> tab selects marchers by what they are rather than where they stand.

<Media
  id="marchers-select-tab"
  alt="The Select tab: a Select by menu set to Section, a button for each section in the show, and Select all."
/>

1. Select the <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" /> tab.
2. Open <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectByText" /> and choose <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.section" />, <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.tag" />, <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.family" /> or <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.drillPrefix" />.
3. Select a group.

The group replaces the selection. <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> a group to add it instead, for example the trumpets and the mellophones together.

<Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.family" /> groups sections into Woodwind, Brass, Battery, Guard, Other and Pit. <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.tag" /> lists the tags you've made; see [Change how marchers look](/guides/marchers/appearance/#make-a-tag). With many groups, a search box appears.

## Select by what they hold

To select everyone holding a rifle at the playhead, whatever their section, right-click a marcher, open <Ui k="field.menu.selectByEquipment" />, then <Ui k="cues.family.guard" />, and choose Select <Ui k="cues.plural.rifle" />. Every item works the same way, and each family's submenu can select all of it, such as <Ui k="actions.cues.selectHoldingFamily.brass" />. See [Change what performers hold](/guides/marchers/equipment/#see-who-holds-what).

## Select one marcher in a placed shape

Clicking a marcher in a placed shape selects the whole shape. To select only that marcher, double-click it or <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="click" /> it. See [Change a placed shape](/guides/shapes/place-marchers/).

## On a tablet

Tap a marcher to select it, and tap empty field to clear the selection. [Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/) has the rules every touch shares. For selecting, two things differ:

- To add marchers, tap <Ui k="touch.modifiers.add" /> over the field, then tap or draw around them. <Ui k="touch.modifiers.subtract" /> takes them out instead.
- What one finger does on empty field depends on the drag mode, the first button over the field. <Ui k="touch.dragMode.auto" />, the starting mode, moves the view with a finger and draws a box with a pen; to draw a box with a finger, hold still for half a second, then drag. <Ui k="touch.dragMode.box" /> and <Ui k="touch.dragMode.lasso" /> draw with a finger, and two fingers move the view. <Ui k="touch.dragMode.pan" /> only moves the view.

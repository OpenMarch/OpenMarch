<InShort>With a show open, select <Ui k="view3d.open" /> at the right end of the toolbar, beside the Editor and Preview switch.</InShort>

The 3D View shows the show from the stands: the band in uniform on a lit field, with instruments, flags and a crowd. It opens in a window of its own and follows the editor, so you can write drill in one window and watch it in the other. To watch from a stadium, choose it under <Ui k="view3d.overlay.venue" plain /> in View settings ([Change the venue and uniforms](/guides/3d-view/venue-and-uniforms/)); to watch from a seat, use <Ui k="view3d.overlay.pickSeat" plain /> in the camera menu ([Move the camera](/guides/3d-view/cameras/#sit-in-the-stands)).

The 3D View opens on the first camera in its camera menu, at the same moment as the editor. While it's open, the <Ui k="view3d.open" plain /> button leaves the toolbar; use your system's window switcher to bring the window forward. You can also open it from the command palette (<Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />), or in the desktop app from **View › Open 3D View** in the app menu.

In the web editor the 3D View opens in a new browser window. If nothing opens, the browser blocked the pop-up: allow pop-ups for the site and select <Ui k="view3d.open" plain /> again.

## Open it with every show

The first time the 3D View opens with a show, it asks <Ui k="view3d.autoOpenPrompt.title" plain />. Choose <Ui k="view3d.autoOpenPrompt.yes" /> to have it open whenever you open a show, or <Ui k="view3d.autoOpenPrompt.no" /> to open it yourself. To change your answer later, turn <Ui k="settings.general.openView3dWithShow" /> on or off at the top of <Ui k="view3d.settings.panelTitle" plain /> › <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.preferences" plain /> in the 3D View, or in [Settings](/guides/reference/settings/#general). The web editor never opens it by itself, because browsers block windows that open without a click.

## Play the show in 3D

The 3D View has no playback of its own: play, pause, scrub or change pages in either window and both move together. The window has the editor's play, previous and next buttons and the playback speed. The editor's keys for playing, pages, counts and speed work in it too (<Kbd action="playPause" />, <Kbd action="previousPage" /> and <Kbd action="nextPage" />, <Kbd keys="Home" /> and <Kbd keys="End" />, <Kbd action="holdSlowerPlayback" /> and <Kbd action="holdFasterPlayback" />); they're explained in [Play the show](/guides/timeline/play-the-show/). <Kbd keys="C" /> is different: in the 3D View it turns the crowd on or off, not the loop. <Ui k="view3d.settings.panelTitle" plain /> (the gear) lists every key the 3D View takes on its <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.preferences" plain /> tab, under <Ui k="view3d.settings.shortcuts" plain />.

Next to the controls, the readout shows the page, the count within the page and the show time. Past the last page it shows how many counts past the end the playhead is.

## See the marchers mid-step when paused

While the show is paused, the marchers stand in the closed position: at attention, feet together, on their dots at that moment, even in the middle of a move. To see them as they actually are at that moment, mid-step, select <Ui k="view3d.pausedPose.label" /> (the footprints button next to the playback speed) or press <Kbd keys="S" /> in the 3D View. Do it again to go back to the closed position. The choice is kept on this computer. While the show plays, the marchers always march.

## Fill the screen

Select <Ui k="view3d.overlay.enterFullscreen" /> in the 3D View, or press <Kbd keys="F" />. In fullscreen the controls fade out when the pointer is still and the readout grows, which suits a projector at rehearsal. Move the pointer to bring the controls back, and press <Kbd keys="F" /> or <Kbd keys="Escape" /> to leave fullscreen.

## If the 3D View is slow

Lower <Ui k="settings.view3d.quality" plain /> to <Ui k="settings.view3d.quality.low" plain />, or <Ui k="settings.view3d.resolution" plain /> to <Ui k="settings.view3d.resolution.fast" plain />, on the <Ui k="view3d.settings.tabs.preferences" plain /> tab of <Ui k="view3d.settings.panelTitle" /> (the gear) in the 3D View. These are kept on this computer, not in the show. When the 3D View drops to Low by itself, a notice says so: choose <Ui k="view3d.quality.useHigh" /> to go back to High and stay there, or <Ui k="view3d.quality.keepLow" />.

If it's still slow on Low, your computer is drawing without its graphics card: update the graphics drivers and restart OpenMarch. In the web editor, turn on the browser's graphics acceleration and restart the browser.

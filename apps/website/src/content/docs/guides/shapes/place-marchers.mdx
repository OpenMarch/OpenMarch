<InShort>Click any marcher in the shape to select the whole shape, then drag its handles or change its numbers in the floating editor. Each change saves when you let go.</InShort>

A placed shape keeps its kind and its numbers, so you can come back and change it as a shape rather than marcher by marcher. To draw one, see [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

## Change a placed shape

Selecting a placed shape brings back its outline, handles and <Term id="floating-editor">floating editor</Term>, with no Place button. Each change is one step you can undo.

<Media
  id="shapes-edit-placed"
  alt="A placed box of trumpets. Clicking one trumpet selects the whole box, showing its outline, handles and floating editor; the box's right side handle is dragged out, the trumpets spread wider, and the change is saved when the button is let go."
/>

Drag inside the shape, or press the arrow keys, to move it. Its handles and numbers work as they did before you placed it; see [Draw a box](/guides/shapes/draw-a-box/#change-the-boxs-numbers) and the other draw pages.

## Select one marcher in a shape

Double-click a marcher in the shape to select that marcher alone. <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="click" /> does it in one click, and so does right-clicking the marcher and choosing <Ui k="actions.shape.enterBox" />. Press <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> to go back to the whole shape.

## Take a marcher out of a shape

Move a marcher off their spot and they leave the shape; a notice says so, and their spot stays empty. In a closed shape, <Ui k="shapes.box.left.closeUp" /> in that notice, or in the floating editor while the shape is selected, lays the marchers left out in the shape again: a box in Grid takes the layout that fits the new count, or Short back, and an outline spreads round again. A box also offers <Ui k="shapes.box.left.resplit" />, which lays it out again for the marchers left. Undo puts the marcher back.

## Ungroup a shape

To stop treating the marchers as a shape, select it and press <Kbd action="shapeUngroup" />, or right-click it and choose <Ui k="actions.shape.ungroup" />. Nobody moves.

## Make a shape from where marchers stand

Select the marchers, right-click one of them and choose <Ui k="actions.shape.group" /> (<Kbd action="shapeGroup" />). Nobody moves. From then on, clicking any of them selects them all, and you can move, turn, flip and scale them together with the shape's handles and floating editor.

## Shapes on later pages

On the pages after, the shape is there too for as long as every one of its marchers stays on its spot, and they're <Term id="held">Held</Term> there: the shape is their hold. You can select it on those pages. Changing it on a later page gives that page its own copy of the shape; the earlier page keeps the one it had.

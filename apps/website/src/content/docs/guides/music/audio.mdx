Add a recording of the show's music so it plays with the drill and its waveform shows on the timeline. The recording is saved inside the show file, so it travels with the show to another computer. OpenMarch opens MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC and WebM files.

## Add a music file

<Steps>

1. Select the music notes button, <Ui k="tempoMode.button.label" />, in the timeline's controls (<Kbd keys="Shift+T" />). The timeline changes to Edit music, with its tools along the top.
2. Select <Ui k="tempoMode.music.add" />. If the show already has music, select its file's name, then <Ui k="tempoMode.music.importAnother" />.
3. Choose the file.

</Steps>

  id="music-add"
  alt="The left end of Edit music's header in a show with no music yet: Editing music, Add music…, Tap tempo and Metronome."
/>

The file's name takes the place of <Ui k="tempoMode.music.add" />, its waveform fills the <Ui k="tempoMode.lanes.music.name" /> lane, and it becomes the recording the show plays. Select <Ui k="tempoMode.toolbar.done" /> or press <Kbd keys="Escape" /> to go back to the timeline.

You can also add music with <Ui k="actions.file.insertAudio" /> in the command palette (<Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />), or when you create a new show.

## Switch to another recording

A show can keep several recordings, such as a full-band recording and a MIDI mock-up. Only one plays at a time. In Edit music, select the music file's name, then the recording you want. A check mark shows the one that plays.

## Set where the music starts

The music offset is how many seconds after count 1 the music starts.

<Steps>

1. In Edit music, select the music file's name, then <Ui k="tempoMode.music.offset" />. The menu shows the current offset.
2. Enter the offset in seconds. For silence or a count-off at the start of the recording, enter a negative number: that much of the recording is skipped. If the drill starts before the music, enter a positive number: the music starts that long after count 1.
3. Select <Ui k="tempoMode.music.offsetSet" />.

</Steps>

The waveform slides under the counts, and <Kbd action="performUndo" /> puts the offset back. The same number is <Ui k="workspaceSettings.audioOffsetSeconds" /> in [Show settings](/guides/field/show-settings/).

To make the first hit land on a count, play the opening with the metronome on ([The metronome](/guides/music/metronome/)) and change the offset until the click and the hit sound together. Then line up the rest with the tempo: see [Set the tempo to match the music](/guides/music/tempo/).

## Delete a recording

<Steps>

1. In Edit music, select the music file's name, then <Ui k="tempoMode.music.delete" />.
2. Select <Ui k="music.delete" />.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">Deleting a recording can't be undone. Keep a copy of the audio file if you might want it again.</Aside>

With no recording left, the show plays in silence. The counts and drill don't change.

## Change the volume or hide the waveform

The volume and muting the music are on the timeline's speaker button: see [The metronome](/guides/music/metronome/#hear-only-the-metronome). To change how the waveform looks on the timeline, or hide it, use <Ui k="settings.timeline.waveformStyle" /> in [Settings](/guides/reference/settings/#timeline). The <Ui k="tempoMode.lanes.music.name" /> lane in Edit music always shows it.

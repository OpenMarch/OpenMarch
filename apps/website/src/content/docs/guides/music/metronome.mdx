<InShort>Press <Kbd keys="Control+M" />, or select the speaker button in the timeline's controls and then the metronome item in its menu.</InShort>

The metronome clicks on every count while the show plays. Use it to hear whether the counts sit on the music, and to rehearse a page at its tempo with the music muted.

## Turn the metronome on or off

The speaker button's metronome item names its state: <Ui k="timeline.sound.metronomeOff" /> until you select it, <Ui k="timeline.sound.metronomeOn" /> after, and a dot shows on the speaker button while it's on. In Edit music, <Ui k="tempoMode.toolbar.metronome" /> in the header does the same.

## Change the volume

The slider in the speaker button's menu sets the volume of the music and the metronome together. To make the click louder or quieter against the music, use <Ui k="music.volume" /> in the <Ui k="sidebar.metronome" /> panel (below).

## Hear only the metronome

Select the speaker button, then the speaker icon in its menu (<Ui k="timeline.sound.mute" />). The music goes silent and the metronome keeps counting. Select it again to bring the music back.

## Change how the metronome sounds

<Steps>

1. Select <Ui k="sidebar.metronome" /> in the sidebar.

   <Media
     id="music-metronome-panel"
     alt="The Metronome panel beside the sidebar, its button highlighted: On/Off off, Accent first beat on, measure-only clicks off, Volume at 50%, and Beat style Standard."
     maxWidth={420}
   />

2. Change any of these:
   - <Ui k="music.onOff" />: the metronome itself, as above.
   - <Ui k="music.accent" />: the first count of each measure clicks louder.
   - <Ui k="music.onlyClickOnMeasure" />: the metronome clicks only on the first count of each measure.
   - <Ui k="music.volume" />: how loud the click is against the music.
   - <Ui k="music.beatStyle" />: <Ui k="music.standard" />, <Ui k="music.sharp" /> or <Ui k="music.smooth" />.

</Steps>

Stop playback to change the accent, measure-only clicks and beat style. The metronome's settings last until you close OpenMarch. Each time you open it, the metronome starts off, at 50% volume, with the first count accented.

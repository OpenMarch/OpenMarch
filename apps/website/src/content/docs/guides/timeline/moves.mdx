<InShort>In Expert, drag the start flag to where the marchers should leave, then drag the marchers on the field; a bar for the new move appears under the music. In Basic, put the playhead on a count and drag the marchers to give them a position there.</InShort>

Every page has a <Term id="move">move</Term> of its own, and that's all most drill needs. In Expert you can also make moves that start or end in the middle of a page, run past a page's flag, or cover only some of the marchers on a page, and change each one's timing on its own. [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/#moves) explains how moves and pages fit together.

Everything on this page except the last section needs Expert mode. See [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

## Make a move

A move covers the counts between the <Term id="start-flag">start flag</Term> and the playhead. When those counts are exactly one page, your changes go into the page's move. When they start or end anywhere else, you get a new move.

1. Select the box of the page where the move ends.
2. Drag the start flag to the count where the marchers should leave. To end the move before the page's set, drag the playhead too.
3. [Select the marchers](/guides/marchers/select-marchers/) and drag them to where they should be at the playhead.

A bar named "Move 1" (or the next free number) appears under the music, from the start flag to the playhead. Up to the start flag the marchers move as they did before; from there they walk to their new spot, arriving at the playhead. If the move ends before the page's flag, they then carry on with the page's move and still reach the page's set. If you see a <Ui k="timeline.lanes.movesHidden" /> strip instead, the <Ui k="timeline.lanes.moves" /> lane is hidden: click the strip to show it ([Lanes](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/#lanes)).

<Media

To set the counts first, hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> and drag along the timeline: the start flag and the playhead go to the two ends of the drag. The drag also turns <Ui k="timeline.loop.label" plain /> on, so Play repeats that stretch; press <Kbd action="toggleLoop" /> to turn it off.

## Move marchers across several pages

1. Select the first page's box, then <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> the box of the last page. The start flag and the playhead now span all of them, and the editing line over the field names the sets in between, for example "through set 5".
2. Drag the selected marchers to where they should be at the end.

The marchers walk straight from where they were to the new spot instead of stopping at each page's set. A message names each page whose flag they now walk straight through, for example "Now walks straight through the end of Page 5", with a button to stop there instead.

## Give some marchers a move of their own

To change the timing of some of the marchers in a move without changing the others:

1. Select those marchers on the field.
2. Right-click the move's bar, or the page's box for a page's move, and select <Ui k="timeline.split.menuNone" />. The menu shows how many are selected, for example "Split 2 selected into new move".

The selected marchers get a new move with the same counts and the same motion, and nothing moves on the field. The new move opens on its own (see [Work on one move alone](#work-on-one-move-alone)), so you can resize or split it without touching the others. Press <Kbd keys="Escape" /> when you're done.

Dragging a move's bar down into the empty space below it does the same for the selected marchers in it.

## Change when a move starts and ends

Drag either end of the move's bar. The move takes more or fewer counts; the marchers still go to the same spots. While you drag, a label shows the counts before and after. Hold <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="drag" /> to put the end on any count.

<Media
  id="timeline-resize-move"
  alt="Move 1's right end is dragged two counts into Page 4, then its left end is dragged back to the start of Page 3. The bar grows each time, and a label shows its counts changing."
/>

An end stops where it would run into another of the marchers' moves, and the label says which. When the start flag or the playhead is on the end you want, dragging there moves the line instead; select another page first to move the lines out of the way.

Drag the middle of the bar sideways to move the whole move earlier or later. To change when a page's move ends, drag the page's flag; see [Change how many counts a page has](/guides/timeline/pages/#change-how-many-counts-a-page-has).

## Use the move's menu

Right-click a move's bar, or select the ⋯ button on it, to open its menu.

<Media
  id="timeline-move-menu"
  alt="The right-click menu of the bar named Move 1, with the playhead inside the move and two of its marchers selected: Edit move, Select marchers, Rename move, Split at playhead, Split 2 selected into new move, Merge into Page 3 fit to its counts (greyed out, with a question mark that explains why), and Delete move."
/>

- <Ui k="timeline.moveMenu.edit" /> opens the move on its own; see [Work on one move alone](#work-on-one-move-alone).
- <Ui k="timeline.moveMenu.selectMarchers" /> selects everyone in the move.
- <Ui k="timeline.moveMenu.rename" /> names the move. You can also press <Kbd keys="F2" /> on a selected bar.
- <Ui k="timeline.moveMenu.splitAtPlayhead" /> cuts the move in two; see below.
- <Ui k="timeline.moveMenu.delete" /> deletes the move. Its marchers go back to what the page's move does, and a message names any page that now looks different, with <Ui k="actions.edit.undo" />.

An item that can't be used right now is greyed out; point at the question mark beside it to see why. The menu works only while the show is paused.

## Split a move at the playhead

1. Pause with the playhead inside the move, not on either end.
2. Right-click the move's bar and select <Ui k="timeline.moveMenu.splitAtPlayhead" />.

The move becomes two moves that meet at the playhead, and the marchers keep their positions at that count, so the show plays as it did. The part after the playhead keeps the move's name; the part before it gets a new name and is selected. If a marcher walked a curved path, its path after the playhead starts again from where it is, and a message says so.

The command palette (<Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />) has the same command as <Ui k="actions.timeline.splitMoveAtPlayhead" />. If several moves cross the playhead, it asks you to right-click the one to split.

A page's own move has no bar to split. To cut it at a count, add a page there with [Create page at playhead](/guides/timeline/pages/#add-a-page-at-the-playhead).

## Merge moves

Drag the move's bar onto another move's bar, or up onto a page's box. The target is outlined with what will happen, for example "Merge into Page 3", and releasing merges them. The move's menu also has <Ui k="timeline.merge.menuInto" values={{ target: "Page 3" }} /> for each page it overlaps.

<Media
  id="timeline-merge"
  alt="Pages 2 to 4 with a bar for four brass marchers' own move under Page 3. The bar is dragged up onto Page 3's box, which is outlined with Merge into Page 3; on release the bar is gone and its marchers are back in Page 3's move."
/>

If the two moves have different counts, the merged marchers are fitted to the target's counts: they reach the same spots, at the target's end. The menu item says so: <Ui k="timeline.merge.menuIntoFit" values={{ target: "Page 3" }} />. A merge is refused, with the reason, when a marcher is in both moves.

## Work on one move alone

Double-click a move's bar or a page's box, or select <Ui k="timeline.moveMenu.edit" /> in its menu. The move is isolated:

- Everyone not in the move is dimmed and can't be selected.
- The move's paths are drawn on the field, and dotted gray paths show where the move would take marchers who left it.
- The loop is on, over the move's counts, so Play repeats it.
- A bar at the top of the field names the move and how many marchers are in it.

<Media
  id="timeline-isolate"
  alt="Move 1 isolated: a bar at the top of the field reads Isolated, Page 3, counts 5 to 10, 4 marchers, with a Done button. Below it the other marchers are faded, and the four marchers in the move have lines back to where the move starts."
/>

Select **Done** in that bar, or press <Kbd keys="Escape" />, to go back.

## Set a position in the middle of a page (Basic)

In Basic, marchers can arrive somewhere partway through a page and still reach the page's set at its end.

1. Click the count on the measure numbers under the page's box to put the playhead there.
2. [Select the marchers](/guides/marchers/select-marchers/) and drag them to where they should be at that count.

The page's move splits at that count for those marchers: they reach the new spot at that count and the page's set at the page's flag. While they're selected, a thick tick in the accent color runs down through the page's box at that count, under a diamond. To take the position away, right-click the tick and select <Ui k="timeline.basic.point.remove" />. When the playhead is on the tick's count, as it is right after you move marchers there, it covers the tick: right-click the playhead instead, and its menu has <Ui k="timeline.basic.point.remove" /> too. On a touch screen, press and hold the tick or the playhead.

If the marchers are <Term id="held">Held</Term> from that count, <Ui k="timeline.basic.point.remove" plain /> is dimmed and says why: remove the hold first.

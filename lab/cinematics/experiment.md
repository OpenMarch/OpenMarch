Open the **3D View** from the **View** tab. In the camera menu at the top left,
choose **Director** (or press **D**). Then press play.

**What to try**

- Watch the whole show with Director on. It is a demo sequence: one shot every
  16 counts, cycling through every built-in shot.
- Pause, then scrub the timeline. The camera should show the same picture at a
  count whether you play or scrub.
- Drag, scroll or press 1–9 while Director is on. You get your own camera back
  where you are; press **D** to hand it back to the Director.

This test is only the camera. Choosing and placing your own shots comes later,
and your answers decide what that looks like.

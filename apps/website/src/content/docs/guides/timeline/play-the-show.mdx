## Where Play starts

Where Play starts and stops depends on the mode:

- In Basic, Play starts at the start of the page the playhead is on and plays to the end of the show.
- In Expert, Play starts at the <Term id="start-flag">start flag</Term> and stops at the playhead. When the two are on the same count, it plays on to the end of the show.

There's no key of its own for playing one page. To play only one page, select its box and press <Kbd action="playPause" />. In Expert, Play stops at the page's flag. In Basic it carries on to the end of the show, so press <Kbd action="playPause" /> again to stop.

## Play and stop

1. Select the box of the page you want to watch.
2. Select <Ui k="actions.playback.play" />, or press <Kbd action="playPause" />.
3. To stop, select the same button again (its tip now reads <Ui k="timeline.transport.stopAndGoBack" plain />), or press <Kbd action="playPause" />.

Because Stop goes back, you can watch a passage as many times as you like without losing your place. Playback also stops by itself at the end of the show, and goes back the same way.

While the show plays, the timeline scrolls to keep the playhead in view, a page at a time. If you scroll the timeline away, it stops following; the <Ui k="timeline.playheadChip.text" /> button at the edge of the timeline brings it back.

## Other ways to play

Point at Play to see each way of pressing it, with its keys. Holding <Kbd keys="Shift" />, or in Expert <Kbd keys="Alt" />, over Play changes its icon to match.

  alt="The tooltip over Play in Expert, listing three ways to play: Play from start flag and stop at playhead with Space, Play from start flag and continue with Alt and Space, and Play from playhead and continue with Shift and Space."
/>

While stopped:

| To | Press | Or |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play from the start flag and keep going past the playhead (Expert) | <Kbd action="playOn" /> | <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="click" /> Play |
| Play from the playhead and keep going | <Kbd action="playPage" /> | <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> Play |

While playing, <Kbd action="playPage" /> or <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> on Stop pauses instead of going back: the playhead stays on the last whole count played. Press <Kbd action="playPage" /> to play on from there; <Kbd action="playPause" /> plays from the start flag again (in Basic, from the start of the page).

On Windows and Linux, the system can take <Kbd keys="Alt+Space" /> for the window menu; <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="click" /> on Play always works.

## Loop a passage

This needs Expert mode: see [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/). Basic has no loop: press <Kbd action="playPause" /> to stop, which goes back, and again to watch the page once more.

1. Set the stretch to repeat: select a page's box, or drag the start flag and the playhead to its ends. Or hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> and drag along the timeline over it, which turns the loop on too, so skip step 2.
2. Select <Ui k="timeline.loop.label" /> (the two arrows next to Play), or press <Kbd action="toggleLoop" />.
3. Press <Kbd action="playPause" />. The passage plays over and over until you stop it.

The loop shows as a bar along the top of the page row. Drag either end of the bar to change what loops; the start flag and the playhead move with it. Press <Kbd action="playPage" /> to play the loop once.

To turn the loop off, select <Ui k="timeline.loop.label" /> again or press <Kbd action="toggleLoop" />. While you work on one move by itself, the loop is always on; see [Work on one move alone](/guides/timeline/moves/#work-on-one-move-alone).

## Change the playback speed

The speed button shows the current speed, such as 1×.

- Select it to go to the next speed.
- Right-click it for a slider from 0.1× to 8×, with the common speeds as buttons.
- Hold <Kbd action="holdFasterPlayback" /> to play at double speed, or <Kbd action="holdSlowerPlayback" /> for half speed, while the key is down.

<Media
  id="timeline-speed-slider"

The music plays at the new speed without changing its pitch. The speed stays until you change it, and the 3D View plays at the same speed.

## Go to a page, measure or rehearsal mark

1. Select the readout (the page and count above the timeline), or press <Kbd action="timelineGoTo" />.
2. Type where to go: a page number such as 12, a measure such as m23, or a rehearsal mark such as C.
3. Press <Kbd keys="Enter" />.

A page puts the playhead on that page's set; a measure or rehearsal mark puts it on the measure's first count.

## Move the playhead with the keyboard

| Keys | Moves the playhead to |
| --- | --- |
| <Kbd action="nextPage" /> | The next page |
| <Kbd action="previousPage" /> | The previous page |
| <Kbd action="nextBeat" /> | The next count |
| <Kbd action="previousBeat" /> | The previous count |
| <Kbd action="lastPage" /> or <Kbd keys="End" /> | The end of the show |
| <Kbd action="firstPage" /> or <Kbd keys="Home" /> | The start of the show (home) |

The previous and next buttons beside Play do the same: on their own they step a page, with <Kbd keys="Shift" /> held a count, and with <Kbd keys="Alt" /> held they go to the start or end of the show. In Expert, what each of these does to the start flag is in [The playhead and the start flag](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/#the-playhead-and-the-start-flag).

## Zoom, scroll and resize the timeline

- Zoom: hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="scroll" /> over the timeline, or pinch on a trackpad. The count under the pointer stays where it is.
- Scroll: scroll over the timeline to move along the show; scrolling up and down moves it sideways too.
- See the whole show: select <Ui k="timeline.fit.label" /> (the two arrows at the right end of the controls), or press <Kbd keys="Shift+Z" />. Select it or press it again to go back to the zoom you had. OpenMarch remembers whether the timeline was fitted and opens the next show the same way.

In Expert you can also change how much room the timeline takes:

- Drag the line along the top of the timeline to make it taller or shorter. Double-click the line to go back to the height that fits its rows. Basic's timeline has one row and no line to drag.
- Select <Ui k="timeline.compact.label" /> (at the right end of the controls) to shrink the timeline to a thin strip, without the measure numbers and with thinner bars for moves. Select it again for the full timeline. The choice is kept for every show.

## On a tablet

Two fingers scroll the timeline and pinch to zoom it. Press and hold Play for the other ways to play, the ones a mouse gets with <Kbd keys="Alt" /> or <Kbd keys="Shift" />. A tap on the speed button opens its slider. The rest of the controls (go to the start or end, step a count, <Ui k="timeline.loop.label" plain />, sound, <Ui k="timeline.touchTransport.fit" plain /> and new pages at the playhead) are under the <Ui k="timeline.transport.more" /> button. See [Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/).

This is a test build of OpenMarch that runs in your browser instead of as a
desktop app. It's the same editor, so you can make and edit a real show.

**What to try**

- Open a demo show, your own `.dots` file, or start a new show.
- Add marchers, make a few moves on the timeline, add music, and play it back.
- Use **Download .dots** (bottom left) to save your show, then open it in the
  desktop app.

**Good to know**

- Nothing is uploaded. Your show stays in this tab, and closing or reloading
  the tab loses unsaved changes.
- Exporting PDFs or videos, plugins and signing in don't work here.

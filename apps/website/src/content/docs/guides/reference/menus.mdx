These menus are in the desktop app. On a Mac they're in the menu bar at the top of the screen. On Windows and Linux, select <Ui k="titlebar.menu" /> at the start of the title bar to open them. The web editor doesn't have these menus; its own <Ui k="web.fileMenu" /> menu, at the start of the title bar, holds creating, opening and backing up shows, and Settings.

The two systems' menus differ: a Mac has an OpenMarch menu with About, Hide and Quit, more items in Edit, and its own Window menu. The page shows your system's menus; use the switch to see the other one. Keys are shown the way each system writes them: on a Mac, ⌘ is Command, ⌥ is Option, ⇧ is Shift and ⌃ is Control.

Items marked Expert are left out of the <Ui k="menu.playback.title" /> menu in [Basic mode](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/). The menus hold only some of the app's commands: [Keyboard shortcuts](/guides/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/) lists every shortcut, and <Ui k="menu.help.title" /> › <Ui k="menu.help.showShortcuts" /> shows them in the app.

<InShort>In the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.fileTab" plain /> tab, select <Ui k="exportCoordinates.exportButton" />, choose the <Ui k="exportCoordinates.coordinateSheets" plain /> or <Ui k="exportCoordinates.drillCharts" plain /> tab, then select <Ui k="exportCoordinates.export" />.</InShort>

Coordinate sheets (dot sheets) list where each marcher stands on every page, measured from the yard lines and hashes. Drill charts draw the field on every page with every marcher on it. Both are PDFs to print or send to your marchers and staff. They need the desktop app; the web editor can't make PDFs.

Both show each marcher's <Term id="set">set</Term>: where they stand at the end of each page. A spot partway through a page isn't on them, so if you want one on paper, add a page there ([Add and change pages](/guides/timeline/pages/)). Props aren't on either.

## Export coordinate sheets

<Steps>

1. In the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.fileTab" plain /> tab, select <Ui k="exportCoordinates.exportButton" />.

2. Select the <Ui k="exportCoordinates.coordinateSheets" /> tab.

3. Choose the options (below). The preview shows a sheet for an example marcher with your choices.

4. Select <Ui k="exportCoordinates.export" /> and choose where to save.

</Steps>

When the export is done, select the message that appears to open the file or folder.

### Coordinate sheet options

- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.includeMeasures" />: adds a column with the measures each page covers.
- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.abbreviateCoordinateDescriptions" />: writes coordinates in short form, such as "S1: 2 out 45", instead of "Side 1: 2 steps outside 45 yard line".
- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.useXYHeaders" />: heads the columns X and Y instead of <Ui k="exportCoordinates.sideToSideHeader" plain /> and <Ui k="exportCoordinates.frontToBackHeader" plain />.
- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.quarterPageLayout" />: fits four marchers' sheets on each sheet of paper, to cut into quarters. It turns on short coordinates.
- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.organizeBySection" />: makes a PDF for each marcher, in a folder for each section. Without it, you get one PDF with every marcher's sheet in score order.
- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.roundingDenominator" /> rounds coordinates to the nearest whole, half, quarter, eighth or tenth of a step.

## Export drill charts

<Steps>

1. In the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.fileTab" plain /> tab, select <Ui k="exportCoordinates.exportButton" />.

2. Select the <Ui k="exportCoordinates.drillCharts" /> tab.

3. Choose the options (below).

4. Select <Ui k="exportCoordinates.export" /> and choose a folder to export to.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="files-export-charts"
  alt="The Export dialog on the Drill Charts tab with Individual Drill Charts and Include notes appendix pages on, View from set to Performers, and a preview of the show's first marcher's chart drawn from the performers' side, with the front sideline at the top."
/>

### Drill chart options

- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.individualCharts" />: makes a chart PDF for each marcher. Each page shows the whole band with that marcher picked out, a box on where they were on the previous page, a circle on where they go next, the paths between them, and their coordinates. Without it, you get one overview PDF with every marcher on every page and no paths.
- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.viewFrom" />: which side the charts show the field from. <Ui k="exportCoordinates.viewFromAudience" plain /> draws it as the editor does, with the front sideline at the bottom. <Ui k="exportCoordinates.viewFromPerformers" plain /> turns the field around, as marchers see it when they face the front: the front sideline is at the top and <Term id="side-1-and-side-2">Side 1</Term> is on the right. The yard numbers turn the way they're painted on the grass, the labels around the field and the drill numbers stay upright, and the printed coordinates don't change. Individual charts start in <Ui k="exportCoordinates.viewFromPerformers" plain /> and the overview in <Ui k="exportCoordinates.viewFromAudience" plain />.
- <Ui k="exportCoordinates.includeNotesAppendix" />: each page's notes are always printed under its chart, cut short if they're long. Turn this on to add pages at the end with the full notes for every page that has notes.

The preview draws a page of your show as it will print: the first marcher's chart on the second page, or the overview's first page.

The charts draw your show's field, with its markings and field image, and the <Ui k="settings.field.gridLines" plain /> and <Ui k="settings.field.halfLines" plain /> you have turned on for the field in the editor.

## Moves past the end of the show

The exports end at the last page's flag. In Expert a move can run past it, and then the dialog says how many moves won't be in the export. Select <Ui k="exportCoordinates.addPagesToEnd" /> to add pages after the last one up to the end of the last move, each as long as the show's <Ui k="workspaceSettings.defaultNewPageCounts" plain /> ([Show settings](/guides/field/show-settings/)), then export. Your pages keep their numbers, and adding is one step you can undo. Or export as it is, without those moves.

## If you can't export

For drill charts, the <Ui k="exportCoordinates.export" plain /> button stays off until the show has at least one marcher, at least one page after the first, and its field settings, and the preview area says which one is missing. Coordinate sheets can be exported as soon as the marchers have loaded.

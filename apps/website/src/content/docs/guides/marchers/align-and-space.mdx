<InShort>Select the marchers and use the <Ui k="inspector.arrange.title" /> section at the top of the inspector: <Kbd action="alignVertically" /> lines them up across the field and <Kbd action="evenlyDistributeHorizontally" /> spaces them evenly.</InShort>

To put marchers in a box, line or arc instead, see [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

Every tool here changes where the marchers stand on the page you're editing. The same commands are in the field's right-click menu and the <Term id="command-palette">command palette</Term>.

## Line marchers up

Select the marchers, then use the first two <Ui k="inspector.marcher.alignHeading" /> buttons:

- The first, <Ui k="actions.alignment.alignVertically" /> (<Kbd action="alignVertically" />), gives every marcher the same front-to-back position, the average of theirs, so they stand in one line across the field. Nobody moves sideways.
- The second, <Ui k="actions.alignment.alignHorizontally" /> (<Kbd action="alignHorizontally" />), gives every marcher the same side-to-side position, so they stand in one line from front to back.

<Media
  id="marchers-align"
  alt="An arc of twelve selected marchers. Alt+V pulls them into one straight line across the field."
/>

## Space marchers evenly

Select three or more marchers. The two at the ends stay where they are, and the others are spaced evenly between them, in the order they stand.

- To space them side to side, select the first <Ui k="inspector.marcher.distributeHeading" /> button, or press <Kbd action="evenlyDistributeHorizontally" />.
- To space them front to back, select the second, or press <Kbd action="evenlyDistributeVertically" />.

<Media
  id="marchers-space"
  alt="A selected line of twelve marchers with uneven gaps. Shift+H spaces them evenly between the two at the ends."
/>

To space them a set number of steps apart instead, type the number in the <Ui k="inspector.marcher.distributeStepsPlaceholder" /> box beside the button before you select it. The marcher furthest to the left as you look at the field (side to side), or furthest toward the back sideline (front to back), stays put, and the others line up that many steps from it. Clear the box to go back to spacing them between the ends.

Spacing doesn't straighten a line: each marcher keeps its other coordinate. Line them up first.

## Flip a group

The last two <Ui k="inspector.marcher.alignHeading" /> buttons mirror the selection about its pivot, which starts in its middle:

- <Ui k="actions.alignment.flipHorizontal" /> (<Kbd action="flipHorizontal" />) swaps its left and right.
- <Ui k="actions.alignment.flipVertical" /> (<Kbd action="flipVertical" />) swaps its front and back.

To mirror the group across another line, such as a yard line or a marcher, move the pivot there first (see [Turn a group around another point](#turn-a-group-around-another-point)). To mirror a copy and keep the original, use [Paste mirrored](/guides/shapes/copy-and-paste/#paste-mirrored).

## Turn and resize a group

Select two or more marchers. The frame around them has square handles on its corners and sides, a round handle above the top edge, and a small yellow dot in the middle: the pivot.

<Media
  id="marchers-rotate"
  alt="A selected block of marchers with a frame and square handles around it, a round handle above it and a small yellow dot in its middle. The round handle is dragged around, and the block turns about the dot."
/>

- Drag the round handle to turn the group around the pivot, in 15° steps. Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="drag" /> to turn by whole degrees.
- Drag a corner handle to stretch or shrink the group both ways. Hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> to keep its proportions.
- Drag a side handle to stretch or shrink it one way. Hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> to scale the other way along with it.
- Hold <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="drag" /> while resizing to resize about the group's center instead of the opposite edge.
- The side or corner you drag lands on a yard line, hash or sideline when it comes within a few pixels of one, and an orange guide names the line. With [coordinate rounding](/guides/marchers/move-marchers/#snap-to-a-grid-of-steps) on, it lands on the grid instead. Either way, the marchers inside keep their proportions. With <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> on a corner, one of its two sides lands. Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="drag" /> to resize finely, still catching lines.

Only positions change: dots and drill numbers stay upright and the same size. One marcher on its own has no handles.

To turn the group by an exact angle, type it in the <Ui k="inspector.marcher.rotation" plain /> box under <Ui k="inspector.marcher.rotateHeading" />. For a placed shape, use the shape's own floating editor instead.

## Turn a group around another point

The pivot is the point a group turns and flips around. It starts in the middle. To move it:

- Select a dot in the pivot grid under <Ui k="inspector.marcher.rotateHeading" /> to put it on a corner, the middle of a side or the center.
- Select <Ui k="inspector.arrange.pickPivotButton" />, then click a marcher or any spot on the field.
- Or hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> and drag the dot. It snaps onto marchers and onto the group's corners, sides and center.

<Media
  id="marchers-pivot"
  alt="With Ctrl held, the pivot in the middle of a selected block is dragged onto the marcher at the block's back-left corner and snaps there, where it becomes a yellow crosshair. Then the round handle above the frame is dragged a quarter turn, and the block turns around that corner marcher."
/>

Once it's out of the middle, the pivot is a crosshair. A pivot on a marcher stays on that marcher. Double-click the pivot to put it back in the middle; it also goes back when you select different marchers.

## Swap two marchers

Select exactly two marchers, then select <Ui k="inspector.marcher.swapMarchers" /> in the inspector, or press <Kbd action="swapMarchers" /> (in the web editor, <Kbd keys="Mod+Shift+S" />, because <Kbd keys="Mod+S" /> [saves a copy](/guides/files/save-and-open/#save-a-copy-or-close-the-show) there). Each takes the other's place on this page. With any other number selected, the inspector doesn't show the button, and the right-click menu and the command palette show Swap greyed out: <Ui k="actions.swap.selectTwo" />.

## Move marchers to where they stand on the next page

Open <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.selectedMarchers" /> at the bottom of the inspector and choose <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.moveToNextPageSpots" />, or press <Kbd action="setSelectedMarchersToNextPage" />. For every marcher at once, use <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.allMarchers" /> in the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab (<Kbd action="setAllMarchersToNextPage" />). It moves them like a drag does. On the last page it's greyed out: <Ui k="actions.batchEdit.noPageAfter" />.

To have marchers stay where the previous page left them instead, use <Ui k="timeline.terms.clearThisPage" />; see [Reset or lock marchers on a page](/guides/timeline/reset-marchers/#clear-a-pages-positions).

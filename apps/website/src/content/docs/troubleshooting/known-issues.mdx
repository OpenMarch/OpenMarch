These are problems we know about in OpenMarch 0.2, with a way around each one. If you run into something that isn't here, please [report it](/guides/start/get-help/).

## Fingers can't move marchers on a tablet

On a tablet, one finger moves the view of the field and two fingers zoom it, but a finger can't select or drag marchers on the field yet. Most other controls work by touch.

What to do: to write drill on a tablet, connect a mouse or trackpad. To check or watch a show, touch is enough.

## Basic can take a marcher out of a move made in Expert

If you split some marchers off a page into a move of their own in Expert, and then in Basic move one of them partway through the page, that marcher goes back into the page's own move. Nothing tells you it happened. Moving it at the end of the page keeps its own move.

What to do: edit marchers you split off in Expert while you're in Expert. If it already happened, undo the change in Basic.

## A drag on a later page can move a marcher on earlier pages

When a marcher is Held from the start of the show, dragging it on a later page inside that hold moves its starting spot, so it moves on every page before too. That's how a hold works: a drag inside it moves the whole hold. While you drag, the hold's line on the timeline lights up back to the start of the show, but no message says so after you let go. In Expert, a drag on a later page of the hold ends the hold there instead; see [Drag marchers that are Held](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/#drag-marchers-that-are-held).

What to do: after a drag like this, check the earlier pages, and undo it (<Kbd action="performUndo" />) if they shouldn't have changed.

## On the Move shows straight lines between pages

A show sent to the On the Move phone app has each marcher's position at every page, and the phone draws straight lines between them. A move that starts or ends in the middle of a page, or a curved move, looks different on the phone than in the editor. The export dialog says so too.

What to do: where the path in the middle of a page matters to your marchers, add a page at that count, so the phone shows that position.

A shape places exactly the marchers you have selected, one on each spot, at the count the playhead is on. This page covers choosing who goes in, who stands where, the spacing, and changing a shape once it's placed. For drawing each kind, see [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/) and the pages after it.

## Place the marchers

<Steps>

1. Select the marchers who go in the shape. The <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" /> tab selects a whole section at once; see [Select marchers](/guides/marchers/select-marchers/).

2. Put the playhead on the count where they should stand in the shape. To write a move into the shape, that's the end of the move.

3. Pick a kind and draw it on the field.

4. Set the spacing in the floating editor (below).

5. Select <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" />, or press <Kbd action="applyShape" />.

</Steps>

Placing is one step you can undo. Every selected marcher gets a spot: there are always as many spots as marchers, so a bigger selection makes a bigger shape or a closer spacing.

The shape is drawn for the marchers selected when you drew it. If you change the selection while it's open, the shape stays as it is, and placing it still places only its own marchers.

## Who stands where

OpenMarch gives each marcher a spot by least travel, so everyone's walks are as short and as even as they can be, measured from where they stand at the start flag (in Basic, the start of the page). This keeps paths from crossing and keeps a line that moves into another line in order.

That choice is made when you draw the shape, and again when you change how many across or deep it is. Moving, turning, stretching or flipping it keeps everyone on their own spot, so the form stays the same form. After such changes, <Ui k="shapes.edit.recalculate.label" /> (<Kbd action="shapeRecalculate" />) works out who goes where by least travel again; it lights up only when it would change something.

For a line, curve or arc, the <Ui k="shapes.edit.order.label" /> menu on the editor's second row also has <Ui k="shapes.edit.order.reverse" />, which turns the order along the line end for end, and, when every marcher comes from one placed line, <Ui k="shapes.edit.order.keep" />.

To check the walks before you place the shape, select the pathways button in the floating editor: it draws a line from where each marcher starts to their spot.

## Spacing and size

Each kind has its own spacing, in steps between neighbors:

- A box has a side-to-side and a front-to-back spacing, which you can lock together.
- A circle has one interval round its ring, which also sets its size.
- A line, curve or arc either fits its marchers to the length you drew (<Ui k="shapes.kinds.line.spacing.fit" />) or keeps the interval you set and takes the length that needs (<Ui k="shapes.kinds.line.spacing.keep" />).

Expand and Shrink in the floating editor make the whole shape bigger or smaller a half step at a time, keeping its proportions and how many marchers are across and deep. Hold either one to repeat it, or drag it. No spacing goes under half a step.

A spacing can be any number of steps, typed as a whole number, a decimal or a fraction (2, 2.5, 2½, 1⅔, 5/3). The shape keeps it exactly. Dragged spacings and the − and + buttons stay on half steps.

## Change a placed shape

<Media
  id="shapes-edit-placed"
  alt="A placed box of trumpets. Clicking one trumpet selects the whole box, showing its outline, handles and floating editor; the box's right side handle is dragged out, the trumpets spread wider, and the change is saved when the button is let go."
/>

- Click any marcher in a placed shape to select the whole shape. Its outline, handles and floating editor come back, with no Place button: every change saves as soon as you let go, and each is one step you can undo.
- Double-click a marcher in the shape to select that marcher alone, or hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="click" />. Press <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> to go back to the whole shape.
- Drag inside the shape, or press the arrow keys, to move the whole shape.

If you move a marcher out of a placed shape, they leave it, and a notice says so. Their spot stays empty. In a closed shape, <Ui k="shapes.box.left.closeUp" /> in that notice, or in the floating editor while the shape is selected, spaces the marchers left over the shape again; a box also offers <Ui k="shapes.box.left.resplit" />, which lays it out again for the marchers left. Undo puts the marchers back.

To stop treating the marchers as a shape, select it and select the ungroup button in the floating editor, whose tip reads <Ui k="shapes.edit.ungroup" />, or press <Kbd action="shapeUngroup" />. Nobody moves.

## Make a shape from where marchers stand

<Ui k="actions.shape.group" /> (<Kbd action="shapeGroup" />) turns the selected marchers into a shape without moving them. From then on, clicking any of them selects them all, and you can move, turn, flip and scale them together with the shape's handles and floating editor.

## Shapes on later pages

When the marchers hold their spots on the pages after, the shape is there too, and you can select it on those pages. Changing it on a later page gives that page its own copy of the shape; the earlier page keeps the shape it had.

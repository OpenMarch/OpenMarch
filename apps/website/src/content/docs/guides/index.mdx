OpenMarch is a free, open-source app for writing marching band drill. You place marchers on the field, set where they go on each page of the show, and play it back with the music, on the field or in 3D. This guide describes OpenMarch 0.2: what each part of the app does, and how to get things done with it.

## How the guide is organized

The chapters follow the app. [The window](/guides/workspace/the-window/) names each part of it. [Marchers](/guides/marchers/add-marchers/) covers adding, selecting, moving and lining up marchers. [Timeline](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/) explains pages, moves and counts, the heart of writing drill in OpenMarch. [Shapes](/guides/shapes/) covers boxes, lines, arcs and circles. Then come [music](/guides/music/counts-and-music/), the [field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/), the [3D View and props](/guides/3d-view/open-the-3d-view/), and [saving, printing and exporting](/guides/files/save-and-open/).

Every shortcut is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/guides/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/), shown with your computer's own keys, and every term the guide uses is in the [glossary](/guides/reference/glossary/).

If something isn't working, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/file-errors/) and [Known issues](/troubleshooting/known-issues/), or [ask for help](/guides/start/get-help/).

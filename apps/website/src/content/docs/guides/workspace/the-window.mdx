When a show is open, the field takes up most of the window. You arrange marchers on it, and the timeline under it holds the show's counts, pages and moves, so you can see and change when everything happens.

## The field

The field shows where every marcher is at the <Term id="playhead">playhead</Term>'s count. Select marchers by clicking them or dragging around them, and drag them to move them. The yard lines, hashes and grid come from the field's settings, which you can change to match where you rehearse ([Edit the field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/)).

The editing line at the field's top left says what your next edit changes, for example "Editing Page 1's move". At the field's top right, a small bar chooses whether a drag draws a box or a lasso, and the field display buttons under it choose what's drawn, such as drill numbers. The zoom controls sit at the bottom right; [Move around the field](/guides/workspace/move-around-the-field/) covers panning and zooming.

## The timeline

The timeline has the playback controls, the pages of the show, its counts and measures, and the music's waveform. The playhead marks the count the field shows: drag it, or play the show, and the marchers move. [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/) explains pages and moves.

## The toolbar

<Media
  id="window-toolbar"
  alt="The left end of the title bar and the toolbar: the menu, the app's name and version, and undo and redo; under them the File, Alignment, Shapes and Select tabs, with the Alignment tab's controls showing."
/>

The toolbar's tabs hold:

- <Ui k="inspector.shape.fileTab" />: open, create, save and close shows, export charts, coordinate sheets and video, and open Settings.
- <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" />: coordinate rounding (the step grid marchers snap to), locking movement to one direction, and placing all marchers where they stand on the previous or next page.
- <Ui k="inspector.shape.shapesTab" />: drawing a box, circle, line, curve or arc and placing marchers in it.
- <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" />: selecting marchers by section, tag, family or drill prefix, or all of them.

<Media
  id="window-toolbar-end"
  alt="The right end of the title bar and the toolbar: the search button, the question mark for help, and Submit bug or feedback in the title bar, and under them Open 3D View and the Editor and Preview switch."
  maxWidth={430}
/>

At the right end, <Ui k="view3d.open" /> opens the show in the 3D View, and the <Ui k="workspace.editor" /> and <Ui k="workspace.preview" /> switch changes between editing the show and watching it (<Kbd action="togglePreview" />). In the tablet layout the tabs are in the title bar instead; see [Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/#the-tablet-layout).

## The sidebar

<Media
  id="window-sidebar"
  alt="The sidebar's column of icons down the left edge of the window, with the Marchers panel open beside it listing the show's marchers by section."
  maxWidth={560}
/>

The sidebar's icons open panels for the show as a whole: <Ui k="sidebar.marchers" />, <Ui k="sidebar.metronome" />, marchers' <Ui k="sidebar.appearance" />, and <Ui k="props.title" />. Set apart at the bottom are the <Ui k="sidebar.mobile" /> export and <Ui k="sidebar.settings" /> ([Show settings](/guides/field/show-settings/)), such as the default tempo and the number the first page starts at. Point at an icon to see its name. <Ui k="sidebar.field" /> puts the field itself up for editing instead of opening a panel.

The music has no panel. It lives on the timeline; see [Add the show's music](/guides/music/audio/).

## The inspector

<Media
  id="window-inspector"
  alt="The inspector with three marchers selected: the Arrange section at the top, with Align, Rotate and Distribute, and the page's counts and measures under it."
  maxWidth={360}
/>

The inspector shows the details of what you've selected. With no marchers selected, it shows the page: its counts and measures. With two or more marchers selected, the <Ui k="inspector.arrange.title" /> section at the top lines them up, turns them and spaces them ([Align, space and turn marchers](/guides/marchers/align-and-space/)). Each page's notes are at the end of the inspector.

## Find a command by name

Every command in OpenMarch can be found by name in the command palette. Select the magnifying glass in the title bar, or press <Kbd action="openCommandPalette" /> (or <Kbd action="openCommandBar" /> when you're not typing in a box), and start typing what you want to do. Commands that can't run right now are dimmed, with the reason beside them, such as <Ui k="actions.shape.noMarchersSelected" />; a command that can't say why isn't listed. Each command shows its shortcut.

Anywhere in the app, a greyed-out button or menu item says why when you point at it, select it, or hold a finger on it. Its shortcut does nothing then, but the button, if it's on screen, lights up and shows the reason for a moment. [Keyboard shortcuts](/guides/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/) lists the shortcuts.

The venue is where the 3D View puts your show: a high school stadium, a college stadium, a domed pro stadium, an indoor arena and so on, with its lighting and crowd. It and the uniforms are part of the show, so everyone who opens the show sees the same thing.

## Change the venue

<Steps>

1. In the 3D View, select <Ui k="view3d.settings.title" /> (the gear). The <Ui k="view3d.settings.title" plain /> panel opens.

2. Under <Ui k="view3d.settings.venueSection" plain />, choose a <Ui k="view3d.overlay.venue" plain />: <Ui k="view3d.kit.hs" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.bighs" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.college" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.pro" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.gym" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.arena" /> or <Ui k="view3d.kit.blank" />.

3. Choose the <Ui k="view3d.overlay.lighting" plain />. Each venue has its own choices: <Ui k="view3d.lighting.day" />, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.dusk" /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.night" /> in the outdoor stadiums, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.day" />, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.night" /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.roofClosed" /> in the Pro dome, and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.house" /> or <Ui k="view3d.lighting.show" /> lights in the gym and the arena.

</Steps>

The field in every venue is your show's field, with its yard lines, hashes and field image (see [Edit the field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/)). In the stadiums, a football field always has both end zones, even when your show's field stops at the goal lines: the 3D View paints the end zones past them, and the marchers stay where you put them. Other fields, such as an indoor floor, never get end zones. Each venue has its own cameras; see [Move the camera](/guides/3d-view/cameras/).

Everything under <Ui k="view3d.settings.venueSection" plain /> is saved in the show file. Each change is a step in the editor's undo history, so <Kbd action="performUndo" /> in the editor window takes it back.

## Crowd and end zones

In a venue with stands:

- <Ui k="view3d.overlay.crowd" /> fills the stands with spectators. Press <Kbd keys="C" /> in the 3D View to turn them on or off. With the crowd on, <Ui k="view3d.settings.crowdColors" plain /> sets the colors the <Ui k="view3d.settings.homeColor" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.settings.awayColor" plain /> sides wear.
- <Ui k="view3d.settings.endZones" /> paints lettering in both end zones, up to 24 characters, in the color you choose. Leave it empty for no lettering. The gym and the arena have no end zones.

## The video boards

The video boards in the <Ui k="view3d.kit.bighs" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.college" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.kit.pro" plain /> venues, and the scoreboard in the <Ui k="view3d.kit.arena" plain />, show your show's name, with its designer and client when you've entered them. Set these in [Show settings](/guides/field/show-settings/). Without a show name, the boards show the file's name.

## Uniforms

<Ui k="view3d.settings.uniforms" /> dresses the band and <Ui k="view3d.settings.guardUniforms" /> dresses the color guard. Choose one of the built-in uniforms for each, such as <Ui k="view3d.uniformPreset.westPoint" /> or <Ui k="view3d.uniformPreset.sash" />. Marchers in guard sections (Color Guard, Flag, Rifle and the other guard sections) wear the guard uniform; everyone else wears the band uniform.

You can't design your own uniform in this version.

## Horns

<Ui k="view3d.settings.brassFinish" /> chooses how the brass look: <Ui k="view3d.brassFinish.brass" /> (gold) or <Ui k="view3d.brassFinish.silver" />. Every brass horn in the show gets the same finish. Woodwinds keep their own: the flutes and piccolos stay silver and the saxophones gold.

<Ui k="view3d.settings.tuba" /> chooses what the tuba section carries: a <Ui k="view3d.tuba.contra" /> held on the shoulder, or a <Ui k="view3d.tuba.sousaphone" /> worn around the body. Sousaphone players wear a beret instead of the uniform's hat, so the bell has room.

## How the marchers look

<Media
  id="3d-marchers"
  alt="A closer view in the 3D View: brass players in uniform with their horns up, drummers with drums, and color guard with flags, standing in their drill positions."
/>

- Each marcher's body and skin tone are chosen from their marcher number, so the same marcher looks the same every time you open the show. At **High** quality, heights vary a little too.
- What a marcher carries comes from their section's name: trumpets, mellophones, baritones, euphoniums, trombones and tubas carry their horns, woodwind sections their instruments, the battery its drums and cymbals, Color Guard and Flag sections a 6-foot flag, and Rifle sections a rifle. Sections the 3D View doesn't know, such as Pit or Other, carry nothing.
- The section's color from [Appearance](/guides/marchers/appearance/) colors drum shells and flag silks. It doesn't change the uniform.
- Marchers you've hidden in Appearance are hidden in the 3D View too.
- Marchers you select in the editor get a ring at their feet, so you can find them in the 3D View.

## How the marchers move

The marchers march the drill as it's written: every move, at the counts it takes, at the tempo of the music.

- While moving, they step on every count and keep facing the front. Moving sideways, they slide; moving toward the back sideline, they march backward.
- Brass play with horns up, mouthpieces at their lips and heads slightly raised; woodwinds play their instruments; the battery plays; and the guard holds their equipment at set.
- While a marcher holds still and other marchers are moving, they mark time. When the whole band is still, everyone stands.
- When you pause, everyone stands in the closed position on their dot, unless you've turned on <Ui k="view3d.pausedPose.label" plain /> ([When the show is paused](/guides/3d-view/open-the-3d-view/#when-the-show-is-paused)).

The 3D View doesn't let you change these: there are no settings for horn moves, facings, equipment work or choreography in this version.

On every page, each marcher is <Ui k="timeline.terms.defined" />, <Ui k="timeline.terms.held" /> or <Ui k="timeline.terms.notDefined" />. The state decides what happens to the marcher on this page when you change an earlier one. Most of the time you don't need to think about it: a marcher you move on a page is Defined there, and one you haven't moved yet is Not defined.

## The three states

### <Ui k="timeline.terms.defined" plain />

You've set the marcher's position on this page. It reaches that spot at the page's flag, wherever it was before. Changing an earlier page changes how it gets here, not where it ends up.

### <Ui k="timeline.terms.notDefined" plain />

Nothing has been decided for the marcher on this page. It stands where it last was, and when you change that earlier position, it moves here too. Every marcher on a new page is Not defined.

### <Ui k="timeline.terms.held" plain />

You've chosen to keep the marcher where it was on the page before. Like a Not defined marcher, it follows changes to the earlier page. The difference shows when you drag it on this page: dragging a Held marcher moves the whole hold, including where it stands on the page before.

Use Held when the marcher's standing still is part of the drill and should stay that way. Use Not defined, the default, for marchers you haven't got to yet.

## How you can tell

Select marchers, and the timeline shows their states:

- A diamond at the bottom edge of the page row marks each count where the selected marchers have a keyframe, a position you set. It's filled when every selected marcher has a keyframe there, and hollow when only some do. A Defined marcher has a diamond at the page's flag.
- A link mark in a page's box means some of the selected marchers have no position of their own on that page and show an earlier one. Point at it to see who. Marchers that haven't moved since the start of the show don't get one.
- A solid bar along the bottom of a page's box marks where the selected marchers are Held. Point at it to see how many.

On the field, a marcher with no position of its own on the playhead's page is drawn with a dashed outline. In the inspector, the selected marchers' section says <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.movesOnThisPage" /> when they're Defined on the page, or names the page where they last moved, as a link that takes you there.

## Define as shown

<Ui k="timeline.terms.defineAsShown" /> makes Not defined marchers Defined where they stand now, so later changes to earlier pages no longer move them on this page. Held marchers stay Held.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Right-click the page's box and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.defineAsShown" />, or select the link mark in the page's box.

Dragging a Not defined marcher on a page also makes it Defined, at the spot you drag it to.

## Hold from previous page

<Ui k="timeline.terms.holdFromPrevious" /> keeps marchers on this page exactly where they are on the page before.

1. Go to the page and select the marchers.
2. Right-click the page's box and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.holdFromPrevious" />, or press <Kbd action="timelineHoldFromPrevious" />.

A marcher that had its own move on the page loses it and stays still for the page. A message says who is now Held, and lists anyone who couldn't be, with the reason: for example a marcher with no position of its own on the page before, or one in the middle of a move that crosses the page's start.

## Clear this page

<Ui k="timeline.terms.clearThisPage" /> takes the marchers' positions off the page, so they're Not defined there again and stand where the page before left them.

1. Select the marchers. To clear the page for everyone, select nobody.
2. Right-click the page's box and select **Clear Page 3** (with the page's number), or press <Kbd action="setSelectedMarchersToPreviousPage" /> for the selected marchers or <Kbd action="setAllMarchersToPreviousPage" /> for everyone.

The pages after it keep the marchers where they were. Where a later page showed the cleared position, the menu also offers **Clear Page 3, Page 4 moves back too**, which lets those later pages go back with it.

## Remove keyframe

<Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" /> takes away one position at one count, where Clear this page works on a whole page. Use it on a keyframe in the middle of a page.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Right-click the diamond at the count and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" />.

The marchers no longer stop at that spot: they walk from the keyframe before it to the keyframe after it. As with Clear this page, later pages keep where they were, unless you choose the second item, which lets the later pages that showed that position move back too.

In Basic, a position in the middle of a page shows as a tick in the page's box; right-click it and select <Ui k="timeline.basic.point.remove" />.

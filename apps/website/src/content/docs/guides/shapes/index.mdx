A shape places the selected marchers at one count, the count the <Term id="playhead">playhead</Term> is on. You draw a box, circle, line, curve or arc on the field, it shows a spot for every selected marcher, and when you place it each marcher stands on a spot.

<Media
  id="shapes-open-box"
  alt="A 4 × 3 box drawn on empty field and not yet placed: twelve spots labeled with trumpets' drill numbers inside a purple outline, square handles on its corners and sides, a round knob above it, and under it the floating editor with the size, spacings and buttons, ending in Place."
/>

Placing writes ordinary positions, as if you'd dragged the marchers there, so the timeline walks them into the shape like any other move. The shape is kept with them, so you can select it later and change it.

## Kinds

| Kind                                       | Key                          | What it makes                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <Ui k="shapes.kinds.block.label" plain />  | <Kbd action="shapeBlock" />  | A block in rows, round the edge of a rectangle, or spread inside one  |
| <Ui k="shapes.kinds.circle.label" plain /> | <Kbd action="shapeCircle" /> | A ring an even interval apart, or a filled circle                     |
| <Ui k="shapes.kinds.line.label" plain />   | <Kbd action="shapeLine" />   | A straight line, or a line with corners through points you click      |
| <Ui k="shapes.kinds.curve.label" plain />  |                              | A line that bends smoothly through its points                         |
| <Ui k="shapes.kinds.arc.label" plain />    |                              | Part of a circle or an ellipse, open or closed                        |

Every kind is in the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.shapesTab" /> tab, greyed out until you select marchers. Curve and Arc have no key, but you can give them one in [Settings](/guides/reference/settings/).

To pick a kind without the toolbar, hold <Kbd action="openShapeRing" /> with the pointer over the field. The shape ring opens around the pointer: slide toward a kind and let go. Tap <Kbd action="openShapeRing" /> instead to keep the ring open until you click. In the tablet layout, tap <Ui k="shapes.tool.tab.ringCaption" /> in the <Ui k="inspector.shape.shapesTab" /> tab.

You can also type a shape: press <Kbd action="openCommandBar" /> or <Kbd action="openCommandPalette" /> to open the <Term id="command-palette">command palette</Term>, type its name, and answer its questions. See [Draw a box](/guides/shapes/draw-a-box/#type-a-box-instead).

## A shape on the field

A drawn shape stays open until you place it or discard it. While it's open, the field shows its outline and spots, each labeled with the marcher who will stand there; rings and faint dots showing where its marchers come from; handles to reshape, turn and move it; and its floating editor, with its numbers and buttons. Everyone else fades to a faint dot.

A shape has exactly one spot for each marcher selected when you drew it, so more marchers make a bigger shape or a closer spacing. Changing the selection while it's open doesn't change who's in it.

Select <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" /> or press <Kbd action="applyShape" /> to place it. <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> discards it, and undo brings it back.

The shape stays open if you go to another count. Away from its count it waits behind a notice on the field, <Ui k="shapes.edit.away.text" values={{ moment: "count 16" }} />, with <Ui k="shapes.edit.away.goBack" /> to return to it.

## Spots, intervals and steps

Spacings are intervals: steps from one marcher to the next. "2 steps" means each marcher is two steps from its neighbor. Every spot sits on the field's half-step grid unless you ask for something finer.

To set a spacing, click the number in the floating editor and type it, or drag it. Typed spacings can be whole steps, halves or fractions: 2, 2.5, 2½, 1⅔ or 5/3, and the shape keeps them exactly. Dragged spacings, and Shrink and Expand, move in half steps. No spacing goes under half a step.

A box or circle is filled: its spots fill an area, in rows, round the edge, or spread inside it ([Draw a box](/guides/shapes/draw-a-box/#choose-how-the-box-is-filled) lists the fills). A line, curve or arc is a path: its marchers stand along it, with one on each end.

## Who goes where

OpenMarch gives each marcher a spot by least travel, so the walks are as short and as even as they can be, measured from where each marcher starts: at the <Term id="start-flag">start flag</Term> in Expert, at the start of the page in Basic. Paths don't cross, and a line that moves into another line stays in order. If the spots look crossed, check where the start flag is.

Who goes where is worked out when you draw the shape, and again when its spots are laid out differently, such as a different number across or deep. Moving, turning, stretching or flipping the shape keeps each marcher on their own spot, so a flipped form is the same form mirrored.

- To work it out again by least travel after a change, select <Ui k="shapes.edit.recalculate.label" /> in the floating editor (<Kbd action="shapeRecalculate" />). It's greyed out when nothing would change.
- On a line, curve or arc, the <Ui k="shapes.edit.order.label" /> menu on the editor's second row also has <Ui k="shapes.edit.order.reverse" />, which turns the order end for end, and, when every marcher comes from one placed line, <Ui k="shapes.edit.order.keep" />, which keeps them in the same order along the new one.
- To check the walks before you place the shape, select the pathways button, the last one before <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" /> on the editor's first row (its tip reads <Ui k="shapes.edit.pathways.show" plain />). It draws a line from where each marcher starts to their spot.

## Placed shapes

A placed shape remembers its kind and numbers: click any of its marchers to select the whole shape and change it. See [Change a placed shape](/guides/shapes/place-marchers/).

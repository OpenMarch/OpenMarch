Open a show and select two or more marchers.

**What to try**

- Look at **Arrange** at the top of the inspector: align, flip, rotate and distribute for the group.
- Next to **Rotation**, hover the cells of the 3×3 pivot grid: the field shows where each one is. Click one to turn around that point.
- Type an angle in **Rotation**, or drag the round handle above the frame: the group turns around the pivot (the yellow crosshair).
- Rest the pointer on the small dot in the middle of the group: a note says how to move it. Hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) and the dot grows; drag it onto a marcher and it snaps there. A plain drag in the middle still moves the group.
- Or select **Pick on field** under Rotation, then click a marcher or any spot. Escape cancels.
- Once the pivot is out, drag it on its own. Double-click it, or drag it back onto the center, to put it back in the middle.
- Move the group: the pivot moves with it. Play the show: the pivot hides.

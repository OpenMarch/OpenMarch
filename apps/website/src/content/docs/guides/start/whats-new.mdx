OpenMarch 0.2 changes how drill is written: pages now sit on a timeline, marchers can move on their own schedule within and across pages, and the field has proper shape tools. Shows from 0.1.7 open in 0.2 after a one-time conversion; [Coming from OpenMarch 0.1](/guides/start/coming-from-0-1/) explains what changes.

## The timeline

Pages, moves, counts, measures and the music sit on one timeline under the field. You set where marchers are at a count, and OpenMarch walks them there. A later page follows your changes to an earlier one unless you've decided otherwise.

## Basic and Expert

Two editor modes. Basic keeps the page-by-page way of working, like a drill chart, with positions in the middle of a page when you need them. Expert shows the full timeline, where a move can start and end on any count, run across pages, cover only some marchers and overlap other moves. You choose when you first open 0.2 and can switch at any time. See [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

## Shapes

Draw a box, circle, line, curve or arc on the field and place the selected marchers in it. Spots snap to half steps, yard lines and hashes, and a placed shape can be edited afterward. See [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

## Edit music

Line the counts up with the music in the timeline: drag the tempo until the counts sit on the beats, tap a tempo, and set tempo changes, fermatas and time signatures. MusicXML import plays out repeats and shows what it found. See [Set the tempo to match the music](/guides/music/tempo/) and [Import a MusicXML score](/guides/music/musicxml/).

## The 3D View

Watch the show from the stands in a window of its own, with marchers in uniform, in a stadium you choose, kept in step with the editor. See [Open the 3D View](/guides/3d-view/open-the-3d-view/).

## Props

Draw props on the field, size and turn them, and move them on the timeline like marchers. In the 3D View they show as plain blocks. See [Add props to the field](/guides/3d-view/props/).

## Seeing and playing the show

- Preview, a watch-only view of the show: [Watch the show in Preview](/guides/workspace/editor-and-preview/).

## Working faster

- The command palette finds any command by name (<Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />).
- Shortcuts can be changed in Settings; [Keyboard shortcuts](/guides/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/) lists them.

## Beyond the desktop

- The web editor runs OpenMarch in a browser, and keeps your shows in the browser's own storage.
- On a tablet, fingers and a pen write drill, in a layout made for touch: [Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/).
- Video export makes a video of the show from the field.

See [Install OpenMarch](/guides/start/install/) and [Export a video](/guides/files/export-video/).

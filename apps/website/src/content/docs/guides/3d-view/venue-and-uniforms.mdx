The venue is where the 3D View puts your show: a high school stadium, a college stadium, a domed pro stadium, an indoor arena and so on, with its lighting and crowd. It and the uniforms are part of the show, so everyone who opens the show sees the same thing.

## Change the venue

<Steps>

1. In the 3D View, select <Ui k="view3d.settings.title" /> (the gear). The <Ui k="view3d.settings.title" plain /> panel opens.

2. Under <Ui k="view3d.settings.venueSection" plain />, choose a <Ui k="view3d.overlay.venue" plain />: <Ui k="view3d.kit.hs" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.bighs" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.college" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.pro" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.gym" />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.arena" /> or <Ui k="view3d.kit.blank" />.

3. Choose the <Ui k="view3d.overlay.lighting" plain />. Each venue has its own choices: <Ui k="view3d.lighting.day" />, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.dusk" /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.night" /> in the outdoor stadiums, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.day" />, <Ui k="view3d.lighting.night" /> and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.roofClosed" /> in the Pro dome, and <Ui k="view3d.lighting.house" /> or <Ui k="view3d.lighting.show" /> lights in the gym and the arena.

</Steps>

The field in every venue is your show's field, with its yard lines, hashes and field image (see [Edit the field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/)). In the stadiums, a football field always has both end zones, even when your show's field stops at the goal lines: the 3D View paints the end zones past them, and the marchers stay where you put them. Other fields, such as an indoor floor, never get end zones. Each venue has its own cameras; see [Move the camera](/guides/3d-view/cameras/).

Everything under <Ui k="view3d.settings.venueSection" plain /> is saved in the show file. Each change is a step in the editor's undo history, so <Kbd action="performUndo" /> in the editor window takes it back.

## Crowd and end zones

In a venue with stands:

- <Ui k="view3d.overlay.crowd" /> fills the stands with spectators. Press <Kbd keys="C" /> in the 3D View to turn them on or off. With the crowd on, <Ui k="view3d.settings.crowdColors" plain /> sets the colors the <Ui k="view3d.settings.homeColor" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.settings.awayColor" plain /> sides wear.
- <Ui k="view3d.settings.endZones" /> paints lettering in both end zones, up to 24 characters, in the color you choose. Leave it empty for no lettering. The gym and the arena have no end zones.

## The video boards

The video boards in the <Ui k="view3d.kit.bighs" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.kit.college" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.kit.pro" plain /> venues, and the scoreboard in the <Ui k="view3d.kit.arena" plain />, show your show's name, with its designer and client when you've entered them. Set these in [Show settings](/guides/field/show-settings/). Without a show name, the boards show the file's name.

## Uniforms

<Ui k="view3d.settings.uniforms" /> dresses the band and <Ui k="view3d.settings.guardUniforms" /> dresses the color guard. Choose one of the built-in uniforms for each, such as <Ui k="view3d.uniformPreset.westPoint" /> or <Ui k="view3d.uniformPreset.sash" />. Marchers in guard sections (Color Guard, Flag, Rifle and the other guard sections) wear the guard uniform; everyone else wears the band uniform.

You can't design your own uniform in this version.

## Flag silks

The color guard's 6-foot flags can have any of the common silk shapes, and you can paint them with your own artwork.

<Media
  id="3d-silk-settings"
  alt="The flag silk rows of View settings: thirteen silk shapes, each drawn as a small outline with its name, with Standard selected; below them, the imported artwork with its picture and file name, Replace and Remove buttons, and PNG, SVG and PDF template buttons for the Standard shape."
  maxWidth={360}
/>

<Ui k="view3d.settings.silkShape" /> chooses the shape: <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.standard" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.curvedTail" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.cSwing" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rainbow" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.arc" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.teardrop" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.distressed" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rounded" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rectangle" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.curvedAngle" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.wings" />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.snake" /> or <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.swing" />. Each shape comes in one common size, such as 36 by 56 inches for the Standard. A show starts with the plain <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rectangle" plain />.

To paint the silks with your own design:

<Steps>

1. Under <Ui k="view3d.settings.silkArtwork" plain />, download the template for the shape you chose with the **PNG**, **SVG** or **PDF** button. The template is drawn at the silk's real size. The pole side is on the left, the grey parts are outside the silk, and the dashed line is the safe area.

2. Paint your design over the template in any image editor, and export it as a PNG. Keep the template's size and proportions, with or without the bleed around the edge.

3. Select <Ui k="view3d.silkArtwork.import" /> and choose the PNG. A large picture is scaled down to 4,096 pixels on its longest side as it comes in, the most detail the 3D View shows, so a template painted at full size imports as it is.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-silk-artwork"
  alt="The band from the front row: the color guard's Standard-shaped flags show the imported design, a white star and a gold stripe on purple."
/>

The flags show your design, cut to the silk's shape. You see it reversed on the back of the silk, the way a one-sided print looks. If you change the shape afterwards, the same picture stretches to fit the new shape, so download that shape's template and paint it again. <Ui k="view3d.silkArtwork.replace" /> chooses another PNG, and <Ui k="view3d.silkArtwork.remove" /> takes the artwork off.

The silks show your artwork instead of the guard uniform's pattern or the section's color. The shape and artwork are saved in the show file, and importing or removing artwork is one step in the editor's undo history.

Some shapes are only approximate:

- <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.curvedTail" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rainbow" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.distressed" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.rounded" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.curvedAngle" plain />, <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.wings" plain /> and <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.snake" plain /> are traced from a chart of common shapes, and their sizes are estimates. Their templates say so: check the cut and size with your flag maker before you print.
- <Ui k="view3d.silkShape.wings" plain /> are worn, but in the 3D View they hang from the flag pole for now.
- The shape and artwork stay the same for the whole show, and every guard member's flag gets them.

## Horns

<Ui k="view3d.settings.brassFinish" /> chooses how the brass look: <Ui k="view3d.brassFinish.brass" /> (gold) or <Ui k="view3d.brassFinish.silver" />. Every brass horn in the show gets the same finish. Woodwinds keep their own: the flutes and piccolos stay silver and the saxophones gold.

<Ui k="view3d.settings.tuba" /> chooses what the tuba section carries: a <Ui k="view3d.tuba.contra" /> held on the shoulder, or a <Ui k="view3d.tuba.sousaphone" /> worn around the body. Sousaphone players wear a beret instead of the uniform's hat, so the bell has room.

## How the marchers look

<Media
  id="3d-marchers"
  alt="A closer view in the 3D View: brass players in uniform with their horns up, drummers with drums, and color guard with flags, standing in their drill positions."
/>

- Each marcher's body and skin tone are chosen from their marcher number, so the same marcher looks the same every time you open the show. At **High** quality, heights vary a little too.
- What a marcher carries comes from their section's name: trumpets, mellophones, baritones, euphoniums, trombones and tubas carry their horns, woodwind sections their instruments, the battery its drums and cymbals, Color Guard and Flag sections a 6-foot flag, and Rifle sections a rifle. Sections the 3D View doesn't know, such as Pit or Other, carry nothing.
- The section's color from [Appearance](/guides/marchers/appearance/) colors drum shells and flag silks. It doesn't change the uniform.
- Marchers you've hidden in Appearance are hidden in the 3D View too.
- Marchers you select in the editor get a ring at their feet, so you can find them in the 3D View.

## How the marchers move

The marchers march the drill as it's written: every move, at the counts it takes, at the tempo of the music.

- While moving, they step on every count and keep facing the front. Moving sideways, they slide; moving toward the back sideline, they march backward.
- When a wind player changes direction, the last step of the old move lands on the ball of the foot, pointing partway to the new direction, and the first step of the new move goes straight into the new path. A full turn around re-steps on the first count instead. You can switch this to a plain blend in Show settings (below).
- Brass play with horns up, mouthpieces at their lips and heads slightly raised; woodwinds play their instruments; the battery plays; and the guard holds their equipment at set.
- While a marcher holds still, they stand. You can have them mark time instead: for the whole show in Show settings (below), or for one hold with its hold style ([Stand or mark time on a hold](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/#stand-or-mark-time-on-a-hold)).
- When you pause, everyone stands in the closed position on their dot, unless you've turned on <Ui k="view3d.pausedPose.label" plain /> ([When the show is paused](/guides/3d-view/open-the-3d-view/#when-the-show-is-paused)).

- Show settings has a <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.title" plain /> section for how the marchers move everywhere in the show:
    - <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.stepOffFoot.label" plain />: the foot every move steps off with.
    - <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.dotMode.label" plain />: with <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.dotMode.foot" plain />, the foot that lands on the last count is on the dot and the marcher centers on it as the feet close; with <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.dotMode.body" plain />, the middle of the body is over the dot.
    - <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.directionChange.label" plain />: how the moving leg gets to a new direction, <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.directionChange.corner" plain /> or <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.directionChange.direct" plain />. Round the corner is the handbook's placement and cut for wind players at 8-to-5; other sizes still blend.
    - <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.holdWhileBandMoves.label" plain />: what a marcher who holds does while others move. It starts as <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.holdWhileBandMoves.stand" plain />. With <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.holdWhileBandMoves.markTime" plain /> or <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.holdWhileBandMoves.highMarkTime" plain />, a holding marcher marks time on the counts where anyone in the band is moving, and stands while the whole band is still. A hold with its own hold style ignores this setting.
    - <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.percussionTravel.label" plain />: <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.percussionTravel.crab" plain /> keeps drummers' hips square to the front, or <Ui k="workspaceSettings.performer.percussionTravel.slide" plain /> moves them like the winds.

Apart from those, the 3D View doesn't let you change these: there are no settings for horn moves, facings, equipment work or choreography in this version.

On every page, each marcher is <Ui k="timeline.terms.defined" />, <Ui k="timeline.terms.held" /> or <Ui k="timeline.terms.notDefined" />. The state decides what happens to the marcher on this page when you change an earlier one. Most of the time you don't need to think about it: a marcher you move on a page is Defined there, and one you haven't moved yet is Not defined.

## The three states

### <Ui k="timeline.terms.defined" plain />

You've set the marcher's position on this page. It reaches that spot at the page's flag, wherever it was before. Changing an earlier page changes how it gets here, not where it ends up.

### <Ui k="timeline.terms.notDefined" plain />

Nothing has been decided for the marcher on this page. It stands where it last was, and when you change that earlier position, it moves here too. Every marcher on a new page is Not defined.

### <Ui k="timeline.terms.held" plain />

You've chosen to keep the marcher still. A hold grows out of whatever last put the marcher there: the keyframe where it last arrived, or its starting spot if it hasn't moved since the start of the show. Like a Not defined marcher, it follows changes to that keyframe. The difference shows when you drag it inside the hold: dragging a Held marcher moves the whole hold, including the keyframe it grows out of, so it stands at the new spot from that keyframe to the end of the hold.

Use Held when the marcher's standing still is part of the drill and should stay that way. Use Not defined, the default, for marchers you haven't got to yet.

## How you can tell

Select marchers, and the timeline shows their states along the bottom edge of the page row:

- A diamond marks each count where the selected marchers have a keyframe, a position you set. It's filled when every selected marcher has a keyframe there, and hollow when only some do. A Defined marcher has a diamond at the page's flag.
- A thin line runs from each keyframe to where the marchers move again, over the counts where they stand still. It's solid where they're Held, and dashed where they're Not defined. Where some of the selection is Held and some Not defined over the same counts, the line is dashed.
- A short upright tick marks where the marchers move again: the start of their next move.
- A shape carried onto later pages draws a solid line too: the shape is what keeps its marchers there.

While you drag marchers on the field, the lines that the drag will change grow thicker and turn the accent color, so you can see which later pages move too before you let go. The same happens while you point at an item such as "Remove keyframe, Pages 4–5 move back too" in a diamond's menu.

On the field, a marcher is drawn with a dashed outline only where it's Not defined at the playhead. Defined and Held marchers, marchers walking in a move, and marchers in a shape carried on are drawn solid. A marcher's label is bold at its keyframes and lighter between them.

In the inspector, the selected marchers' section says what decides where they are on the playhead's page:

- <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.movesOnThisPage" /> when they're Defined on the page.
- <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.notDefinedShowing" values={{ notDefined: "Not defined", page: 9 }} /> when they're Not defined, with the page they last moved on, or <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.notDefinedFromStart" values={{ notDefined: "Not defined" }} /> when they haven't moved since the start.
- <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.heldSince" values={{ held: "Held", page: 3 }} /> when they're Held, with the page where the hold starts, or <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.heldFromStart" values={{ held: "Held" }} />.
- <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.holdFrom" values={{ page: 9 }} /> when some are Held and some Not defined.

The page or the start is a link that takes you there. When only some of the selection is Held, a second line says how many, for example <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.heldOfSelected" values={{ count: 1, total: 2, held: "Held" }} plain />.

## Define as shown

<Ui k="timeline.terms.defineAsShown" /> makes Not defined marchers Defined where they stand now, so later changes to earlier pages no longer move them on this page. Held marchers stay Held.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Right-click the page's box, or the dashed line on it, and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.defineAsShown" />.

Dragging a Not defined marcher on a page also makes it Defined, at the spot you drag it to.

## Hold from last keyframe

<Ui k="timeline.terms.holdFromPrevious" /> keeps marchers still on this page, where their last keyframe put them.

1. Go to the page and select the marchers.
2. Right-click the page's box, or a selected marcher on the field, and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.holdFromPrevious" />. You can also press <Kbd action="timelineHoldFromPrevious" />, or find it in the command palette (<Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />).

While you point at the command, before you select it, the timeline shows the hold it would make: the page row fills from the keyframe the hold grows out of to the end of the page, then pulses.

A marcher that had its own move on the page loses it and stays still for the page. When the pages before it are Not defined for the marcher, the hold reaches back over them to the last keyframe, or to the start of the show for a marcher that hasn't moved, so it's one hold over all those pages. A hold that touches another hold of the same marcher joins it. A message says who is now Held and on which pages, and lists anyone who couldn't be, with the reason: for example a marcher in a shape or following the leader on the page, one whose last move is a shape's move, or one in the middle of a move that crosses the page's start.

## Change how long a hold lasts

A hold's end can be moved: marchers can hold longer, or leave earlier. Its start can't, because it's where the marchers got there.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Point at the right end of the solid line. The pointer becomes a resize arrow and a small handle shows.
3. Drag the end earlier or later.

While you drag, a label says where the hold will end, such as <Ui k="timeline.holdEdge.readoutEnd" values={{ held: "Held", page: 4, count: 2 }} plain />, and what stops it: a hold can't run past the marchers' next move or the end of the show, and lasts at least one count. The end lands on page flags, the playhead and measures when it's near one; hold <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="drag" /> to put it on any count. Press <Kbd keys="Escape" /> to put it back.

To end a hold at a count, right-click the solid line at that count and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.splitHold" />. After the hold ends, the marchers are Not defined: a drag there starts a move from where the hold ends, so they leave early.

Each selected marcher's hold changes, so you can select one marcher and shorten only its hold.

## Drag marchers that are Held

A drag on a page inside a hold moves the whole hold, including the keyframe it grows out of: the marchers stand at the new spot from that keyframe, or from the start of the show, to the end of the hold. While you drag, the whole line lights up.

In Expert, the start flag decides it. A drag whose start flag is at or before the hold's start moves the whole hold, as above; selecting the hold's first page puts the start flag there. A drag whose start flag is later than the hold's start, such as on a later page of a hold over several pages, ends the hold at the start flag and adds a move from there to the playhead. While you drag, the line stays dark before the start flag and lights from it on, so you can see which will happen.

## Hold from start flag to playhead

This needs Expert mode: see [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

<Ui k="timeline.terms.holdOverWindow" /> keeps the selected marchers still over the counts between the start flag and the playhead, even where that's partway through pages.

1. Put the start flag and the playhead around the counts to hold.
2. Select the marchers.
3. Right-click a selected marcher on the field and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.holdOverWindow" />, or find it in the command palette. It has no shortcut.

As with Hold from last keyframe, pointing at the command shows the hold before you select it, and where the marchers are Not defined before the start flag, the hold reaches back over those pages to their last keyframe. Their moves between the two flags are taken out. A move that crosses the start flag is cut there. A move that crosses the playhead keeps its destination, so the marchers walk the rest of it faster, and a step size warning shows if it's too fast. Marchers in a shape or following the leader are left alone, and a message names them.

## Clear this page

<Ui k="timeline.terms.clearThisPage" /> takes the marchers' positions off the page, so they're Not defined there again and stand where the page before left them. It also takes away a hold on the page, so it's how you remove one.

1. Select the marchers. To clear the page for everyone, select nobody.
2. Right-click the page's box and select <Ui k="actions.batchEdit.clearPage" values={{ page: 3 }} /> (with the page's number, and "(all marchers)" after it when nobody is selected), or press <Kbd action="setSelectedMarchersToPreviousPage" /> for the selected marchers or <Kbd action="setAllMarchersToPreviousPage" /> for everyone.

The pages after it keep the marchers where they were. Where a later page showed the cleared position, the menu also offers an item such as "Clear Page 3, Page 4 moves back too", which lets those later pages go back with it. A hold that grew out of the cleared page's keyframe goes too, and the message says so.

## Remove keyframe

<Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" /> takes away one position at one count, where Clear this page works on a whole page. Use it on a keyframe in the middle of a page.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Right-click the diamond at the count and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" />.

The marchers no longer stop at that spot: they walk from the keyframe before it to the keyframe after it. As with Clear this page, later pages keep where they were, unless you choose the second item, which lets the later pages that showed that position move back too; while you point at it, the lines of those pages light up. Removing the keyframe a hold grows out of removes the hold too.

In Basic, a position in the middle of a page shows as a tick in the page's box; right-click it and select <Ui k="timeline.basic.point.remove" />.

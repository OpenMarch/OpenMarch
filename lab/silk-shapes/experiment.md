Open the **3D View** from the top bar, then **View settings** (the gear button
in the 3D View). Scroll to **Flag silk shape** under **Guard uniform**.

**What to try**

- Pick a few shapes, and press Play to watch the color guard's flags wave.
- Under **Flag silk artwork**, download a template (PNG, SVG or PDF) for the shape
  you picked. Paint on it in any drawing app, keeping the page size, and save it
  as a PNG.
- Choose **Import PNG…** and pick your painted file. Then change the shape:
  the artwork is re-cut to the new shape.
- **Remove** the artwork, then undo it from the editor (Ctrl+Z or ⌘Z).

## Clear a page's positions

<Ui k="timeline.terms.clearThisPage" /> takes the marchers' positions off the page, so they're Not defined there again and stand where the page before left them. It also takes away a hold on the page, so it's how you remove one.

1. Select the marchers. To clear the page for everyone, select nobody.
2. Right-click the page's box and select <Ui k="actions.batchEdit.clearPage" values={{ page: 3 }} /> (with the page's number, and "(all marchers)" after it when nobody is selected), or press <Kbd action="setSelectedMarchersToPreviousPage" /> for the selected marchers or <Kbd action="setAllMarchersToPreviousPage" /> for everyone.

The same command is in the inspector, as <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.placeSelectedMarchers" /> › <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.toPreviousPagePositions" />, and for every marcher in the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab, under <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.placeAllMarchers" />.

The first page has no page before it, so it can't be cleared: there the command is greyed out and says <Ui k="actions.batchEdit.noPageBefore" />.

A hold that grew out of the cleared page's keyframe goes too, and a message says so. Later pages stay where they were, unless you choose the item ending in "move back too"; see [Later pages keep where they were](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/#later-pages-keep-where-they-were).

## Remove one position

<Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" /> takes away one position at one count, where Clear this page works on a whole page. Use it on a <Term id="keyframe">keyframe</Term> in the middle of a page.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Right-click the diamond at the count and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" />.

The marchers no longer stop at that spot: they walk from the keyframe before it to the keyframe after it. Removing the keyframe a hold grows out of removes the hold too. In Basic, a position in the middle of a page is a tick instead; see [Set a position in the middle of a page](/guides/timeline/moves/#set-a-position-in-the-middle-of-a-page-basic).

## Lock marchers where they stand

<Ui k="timeline.terms.defineAsShown" /> makes Not defined marchers Defined where they stand now, so later changes to earlier pages no longer move them on this page. Held marchers stay Held.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Right-click the page's box, or the dashed line on it, and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.defineAsShown" />.

Dragging a Not defined marcher also makes it Defined, at the spot you drag it to.

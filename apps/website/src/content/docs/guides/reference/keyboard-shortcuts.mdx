These are OpenMarch's default shortcuts, grouped the way the app's own list groups them. Keys are shown for your system: on a Mac, ⌘ is Command, ⌥ is Option and ⇧ is Shift; on Windows and Linux, the same shortcuts use Ctrl and Alt. Shortcuts marked Expert only work in [Expert mode](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

In the app, press <Kbd action="showShortcuts" /> or choose <Ui k="menu.help.title" /> › <Ui k="menu.help.showShortcuts" /> to see the list, with any keys you have changed. To find a command and its shortcut by name, press <Kbd action="openCommandPalette" /> and start typing.

Some shortcuts in the table are held while you drag, scroll or click; those show the gesture after the keys.

## Change a shortcut

1. Open <Ui k="settings.title" /> (<Kbd action="openSettings" />) and select <Ui k="settings.shortcuts" />.
2. Find the action in the list, or type part of its name in <Ui k="settings.shortcuts.search" />.
3. Point at the row and select the + after its keys, then press the new keys. Press <Kbd keys="Esc" /> to stop without changing anything.
4. To remove one of an action's shortcuts, point at it and select its ×. A row you've changed gets an arrow at its end that puts its default back, and <Ui k="settings.shortcuts.resetAll" /> puts every shortcut back.

If the keys you press already belong to another action, the app says which one. Select <Ui k="settings.shortcuts.reassign" /> to move them to the new action, or <Ui k="settings.shortcuts.cancel" /> to keep them where they were. Changes apply right away.

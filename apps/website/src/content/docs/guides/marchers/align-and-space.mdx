When several marchers are selected, the <Ui k="inspector.arrange.title" /> section at the top of the inspector has the tools that arrange them as a group: line them up, flip them, turn them and space them evenly. Further down, the marcher section can swap two of them and place them where they stand on another page. The frame around the selection on the field has handles to turn and resize it. To place marchers in a box, line or arc instead, use [Shapes](/guides/shapes/).

  alt="The inspector's Arrange section for eight selected marchers: four Align buttons, Rotate with a 3 by 3 pivot grid beside a Rotation box, and two Distribute buttons each with a Steps box."

Every tool here changes the marchers' positions on the page at the playhead, and each one is a single step you can undo with <Kbd action="performUndo" />. The same commands are on the field's right-click menu and in the command palette.

## Line marchers up

Select the marchers, then use one of the <Ui k="inspector.marcher.alignHeading" /> buttons in the inspector:

- <Ui k="actions.alignment.alignVertically" /> (<Kbd action="alignVertically" />) moves every marcher to the same front-to-back position, the average of theirs, so they stand in one line across the field. Nobody moves sideways.
- <Ui k="actions.alignment.alignHorizontally" /> (<Kbd action="alignHorizontally" />) moves every marcher to the same side-to-side position, so they stand in one line from front to back. Nobody moves forward or back.

/>

## Space marchers evenly

Distributing changes the spacing between three or more marchers along one direction. The marchers at the two ends stay where they are, and the others are spaced evenly between them, in the order they already stand.

- To space them side to side, select the first button under <Ui k="inspector.marcher.distributeHeading" />, or press <Kbd action="evenlyDistributeHorizontally" />.
- To space them front to back, select the second button, or press <Kbd action="evenlyDistributeVertically" />.

To space them a set distance apart instead, type the number of steps in the <Ui k="inspector.marcher.distributeStepsPlaceholder" /> box next to the button before you select it. The marcher furthest to the left of the field as you see it (for side to side), or furthest toward the back sideline (for front to back), stays put, and the others are placed that many steps apart from it. Clear the box to go back to spacing them between the ends.

Distributing doesn't straighten a line: marchers keep their other coordinate. Line them up first if they aren't already.

## Flip a group

The last two <Ui k="inspector.marcher.alignHeading" /> buttons mirror the selection about its own middle:

- <Ui k="actions.alignment.flipHorizontal" /> (<Kbd action="flipHorizontal" />) swaps the left and right of the group.
- <Ui k="actions.alignment.flipVertical" /> (<Kbd action="flipVertical" />) swaps its front and back.

## Turn and resize a group

Select two or more marchers. The frame around them has square handles on its corners and sides, and a round one above the top edge. A small yellow dot in the middle is the pivot: the point the group turns around.

<Media
  id="marchers-rotate"
  alt="A selected block of marchers with a frame and handles around it, and a yellow crosshair in its middle. The handle above the frame is dragged around, and the block turns about the crosshair at its center."
/>

- Drag the round handle above the frame to turn the group around the pivot. It turns in 15° steps. Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="drag" /> to turn in whole degrees, or turn <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.snapToggleCaption" /> off in the <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab to turn freely.
- Drag a corner handle to stretch or shrink the group in both directions. Hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> to keep its proportions.
- Drag a side handle to stretch or shrink it in one direction only. Hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> to scale the other direction along with it.
- Hold <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="drag" /> while resizing to resize from the group's center instead of the opposite edge.
- Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="drag" /> while resizing for fine control.

Only positions change: dots and drill numbers stay upright and the same size. Dragging a handle past the opposite side stops the group at its smallest size instead of flipping it. One marcher on its own has no handles.

To turn the group around another point, move the pivot first:

- Select a dot in the pivot grid under <Ui k="inspector.marcher.rotateHeading" /> in the <Ui k="inspector.arrange.title" /> section to put it on a corner, a side's middle or the center of the group. Pointing at a dot shows where that is on the field.
- Select <Ui k="inspector.arrange.pickPivotButton" /> under the <Ui k="inspector.marcher.rotation" plain /> box, then click a marcher or any spot on the field to put the pivot there. Press <Kbd keys="Escape" /> to stop without moving it.
- Or hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> and drag the pivot out of the middle of the group. A plain drag there still moves the group. Rest the pointer on the dot to see a reminder, and hold the key over it to see the dot grow before you drag. Either way, the pivot snaps onto any marcher and onto the group's corners, side middles and center, and otherwise to the half-step grid. Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" /> as well, or turn <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.snapToggleCaption" /> off, to place it freely.

Once it's out of the middle, the pivot is drawn as a crosshair, and you can drag it again without holding a key. A pivot on a marcher or a point of the group stays there as the group moves. One on any other spot moves with the group when you drag it, and goes back to the middle when you go to another page. It also goes back to the middle when you select different marchers, drag it back onto the middle, or double-click it. It's hidden while the show plays.

To turn the group by an exact angle, type an angle in the <Ui k="inspector.marcher.rotation" plain /> box under <Ui k="inspector.marcher.rotateHeading" /> in the <Ui k="inspector.arrange.title" /> section, or drag across it. It's off while a shape is open or a placed shape is selected; turn the shape in its own editor then. The group turns around the pivot as you change the angle, and the turn is saved when you leave the box.

## Swap two marchers

Select exactly two marchers, then select <Ui k="inspector.marcher.swapMarchers" /> in the inspector, or press <Kbd action="swapMarchers" />. Each takes the other's position on this page. With any other number selected, the inspector doesn't show the button, and the field's right-click menu and the command palette show Swap greyed out, saying <Ui k="actions.swap.selectTwo" />.

## Place marchers where they stand on another page

The <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.placeSelectedMarchers" /> menu at the bottom of the inspector, and <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.placeAllMarchers" /> in the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab for every marcher at once, put marchers back where they stand on a neighboring page:

- <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.toNextPageSpots" /> moves the marchers to where they stand on the next page (<Kbd action="setSelectedMarchersToNextPage" /> for the selected marchers, <Kbd action="setAllMarchersToNextPage" /> for all). It's an ordinary move of the marchers, like a drag: on a page where they're [Held](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/), it moves the whole hold. It's also in the field's right-click menu, as <Ui k="actions.batchEdit.moveSelectedToNextSpots" />. On the last page it's greyed out: <Ui k="actions.batchEdit.noPageAfter" />.
- <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.toPreviousPagePositions" /> in the inspector, and <Ui k="actions.batchEdit.clearPage" values={{ page: "N" }} /> in the toolbar menu, run <Ui k="timeline.terms.clearThisPage" />: the marchers stay where the previous page left them, and the page no longer has positions of its own for them. [Defined, Held and Not defined](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/) explains what that means for later pages. Later pages keep where they were; when some of them showed this page's positions, the toolbar menu also offers a second item ending in "move back too", which lets those pages move back with it.

<Steps>

1. Go to the page you want to change.

2. Select the marchers, or skip this to change every marcher with the toolbar menu.

3. Open <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.placeSelectedMarchers" /> in the inspector, or <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.placeAllMarchers" /> in the <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab, and choose the page.

</Steps>

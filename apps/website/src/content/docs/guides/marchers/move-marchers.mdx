<InShort>Drag the selected marchers on the field, or nudge them a step at a time with the arrow keys.</InShort>

Dragging or nudging changes where the marchers stand on the page you're editing, which the <Term id="editing-line">editing line</Term> names, for example "Editing Page 1's move". [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/) explains pages and moves.

## Drag marchers

Drag a marcher to move it. To move several together, [select them](/guides/marchers/select-marchers/) and drag any one of them, or drag inside the frame around them. Dragging a marcher that isn't selected selects it and moves it alone.

<Media
  id="marchers-move-drag"
  alt="A selected row of marchers is dragged toward the back of the field. Then, with Shift held, the row is dragged sideways and moves more slowly than the pointer."
/>

While you drag:

- With [coordinate rounding](#snap-to-a-grid-of-steps) off, the marchers land on a yard line, hash or sideline when their edge or center comes within a few pixels of one, and an orange guide names the line, such as "On 50". With rounding on, the grid decides instead.
- Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="drag" /> for fine control. The marchers move at a quarter of the pointer's speed and ignore [coordinate rounding](#snap-to-a-grid-of-steps), though they still catch yard lines and hashes. You can press and release Shift in the middle of a drag; the marchers don't jump.
- Hold <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> to keep the move straight: across the field or front to back, whichever way you've dragged further.

Where the marchers are <Term id="held">Held</Term>, a drag moves the whole hold; see [Drag marchers that are Held](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/#drag-marchers-that-are-held).

### On a tablet

Drag the marchers with one finger. The buttons over the field, such as <Ui k="touch.modifiers.fine" />, stand in for the keys. [Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/) covers what a second finger does during a drag.

## Nudge with the keyboard

Select marchers, then press an arrow key or <Kbd keys="W" />, <Kbd keys="A" />, <Kbd keys="S" />, <Kbd keys="D" />. Up moves them toward the back sideline, down toward the front.

| Keys                                     | Moves the selection                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <Kbd keys="Up" /> or <Kbd keys="W" />    | One step, landing on a whole step in that direction; one grid step, landing on the grid, when [coordinate rounding](#snap-to-a-grid-of-steps) is on |
| <Kbd keys="Alt+Up" />                    | The same distance, exactly, from where the marchers are           |
| <Kbd keys="Shift+Up" />                  | ¼ step, without snapping                                         |
| <Kbd keys="Mod+Up" />                    | 4 steps, landing on a multiple of 4 steps from the center of the field |
| <Kbd keys="Mod+Alt+Up" />                | Exactly 4 steps                                                  |
| <Kbd keys="Mod+Shift+Up" />              | ⅒ step, without snapping                                         |

The same keys work with the other arrows. <Kbd keys="W" />, <Kbd keys="A" />, <Kbd keys="S" /> and <Kbd keys="D" /> work on their own and with <Kbd keys="Alt" /> or <Kbd keys="Shift" />, but not with <Kbd keys="Mod" />: use the arrows for 4 steps and ⅒ step.

## Nudge with the move pad

In the tablet layout, the inspector shows a <Ui k="inspector.marcher.move.title" /> pad for the selected marchers. Choose how far each press goes, <Ui k="inspector.marcher.move.quarterStep" />, <Ui k="inspector.marcher.move.oneStep" /> or <Ui k="inspector.marcher.move.fourSteps" />, then select an arrow. The pad always moves exactly that far and never snaps. Each arrow is labeled with the way it goes: <Ui k="inspector.marcher.move.back" plain />, <Ui k="inspector.marcher.move.front" plain />, or the name of a side.

## Snap to a grid of steps

In a new show nothing snaps: a dragged marcher goes exactly where the pointer puts it, and an arrow key lands it on a whole step. Coordinate rounding makes dragged marchers land on a grid of steps instead, for example every half step across the field and every step front to back.

<Media
  id="marchers-coordinate-rounding"
  alt="The Alignment tab with X set to a half step, and the Coordinate Rounding menu open below it: an Enable Offset button, the note Does not yet work with groups, and a row of choices for the X axis and another for the Y axis, None, a quarter, a half, 1, 2 and Custom."
/>

1. In the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab, open <Ui k="coordinateRoundingSettings.title" />.
2. For each axis, choose <Ui k="coordinateRoundingSettings.none" />, ¼, ½, 1 or 2 steps, or <Ui k="coordinateRoundingSettings.custom" /> to type a size. X is across the field, side to side; Y is front to back.
3. To shift the grid off the yard lines and hashes, select <Ui k="coordinateRoundingSettings.enableOffset" /> and enter how many steps to shift it by.

The X and Y buttons next to the menu show the current sizes. Click one to step to the next size, or right-click it to go back one.

With rounding on, one marcher snaps onto the grid as you drag it. Several marchers move in whole grid steps together, so each keeps its distance from the others, even if they started off the grid. The menu's note "<Ui k="coordinateRoundingSettings.groupNotice" plain />" is out of date: groups round this way. Resizing a group with its frame's handles lands the side you drag on the grid ([Turn and resize a group](/guides/marchers/align-and-space/#turn-and-resize-a-group)).

To put marchers that are off the grid onto it, select them and select <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.snapCaption" /> in the <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab, or press <Kbd action="snapToNearestCustomFraction" />. Each one moves to the nearest grid point.

## Turn snapping off

To drag without rounding for a moment, hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="drag" />. To turn all snapping off for a while, yard lines and hashes included, select <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.snapToggleCaption" /> (the magnet) in the <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab, or press <Kbd action="toggleSnap" />; [Snapping and modifier keys](/guides/shapes/snapping-and-modifier-keys/#turn-snapping-off) says what it turns off and when it comes back on.

## Move in one direction only

The two lock buttons in the <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab, next to <Ui k="coordinateRoundingSettings.title" />, keep marchers from moving along one axis until you turn the lock off:


- <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.lockXCaption" /> (the up-and-down arrows, <Kbd action="lockX" />) keeps every marcher's side-to-side position, so drags move them only front to back.
- <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.lockYCaption" /> (the side-to-side arrows, <Kbd action="lockY" />) keeps their front-to-back position, so drags move them only across the field.

Each key is the letter of the axis the marchers can still move along. A lock button is highlighted while it's on. The locks apply to dragging marchers and to <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.snapCaption" />, not to the handles that turn and resize a selection. For a single straight drag, holding <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="drag" /> is quicker than a lock.

The words this guide uses, in alphabetical order. Each one means the same thing on every page.

## 3D View

A window that shows the show from the stands, with marchers in uniform on a field in a stadium. You watch in it; you write drill in the editor. See [Open the 3D View](/guides/3d-view/open-the-3d-view/).

## 8 to 5

A step size: eight steps for every five yards, so each step is 22.5 inches. Most drill is written in 8 to 5. A larger step size, such as 6 to 5, covers the same distance in fewer, longer steps.

## Arc

A shape that places marchers along part of a circle. See [Draw an arc](/guides/shapes/draw-an-arc/).

## Back sideline

The sideline farthest from the audience. Its opposite is the front sideline.

## Basic

The editor mode that works in pages, like a drill chart: you set each page, and marchers hold until you move them again. See [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

## Box

A shape that places marchers in rows and columns, given as across × deep, such as 8 × 4. See [Draw a box](/guides/shapes/draw-a-box/).

## Command palette

The box that finds any command by name and runs it. Open it with <Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />, or <Kbd action="openCommandBar" /> when you're not typing in a box; both open the same box, which the shortcut list also calls the command bar. A command that needs numbers, such as a shape's size, asks for them there one at a time. See [The window](/guides/workspace/the-window/#find-a-command-by-name).

## Coordinate sheet

A page for one marcher listing where it stands on every page, as steps from the nearest yard line and hash. Also called a dot sheet. See [Print coordinate sheets and drill charts](/guides/files/print-charts/).

## Count

One beat of the show's music. Moves, pages and the playhead all fall on counts.

Also called a beat.

## Defined

A marcher's state on a page where you've set its position: it has a keyframe on that page, at the page's flag or partway through it. See [Defined, Held, Not defined](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/).

## Drag mode

On a tablet, what one finger or the pen does on empty field: move the view, or draw a box or lasso to select. You choose it with the first button over the field. See [Select marchers](/guides/marchers/select-marchers/#on-a-tablet).

## Drill chart

A printed picture of the field at one page's set, with every marcher's position. See [Print coordinate sheets and drill charts](/guides/files/print-charts/).

## Drill number

A marcher's label on the field and in charts, such as T3 or F12: the section's prefix and a number.

## Editing line

The line of text over the field that says what a drag will change, such as "Editing Page 3's move". See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/).

## Editor

The view for making changes to the show. Its opposite is Preview.

## Expert

The editor mode with the full timeline: moves that cross pages, overlapping moves, the start flag and the loop. See [Basic and Expert](/guides/start/basic-and-expert/).

## Fermata

A note held longer than the beat. In OpenMarch it's one count that lasts as long as you set, without changing the tempo around it; Edit music calls it a hold. It isn't a marchers' hold. See [Set the tempo to match the music](/guides/music/tempo/).

## Field

The playing surface and its markings: sidelines, yard lines, hashes and numbers. Each show has its own field, which you can change. See [Edit the field](/guides/field/edit-the-field/).

## Floating editor

The small bar of numbers and menus that floats beside a selected shape on the field: its size, interval, fill and pivot. See [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

## Front ensemble

The players who stand in front of the field with keyboards, drums and other equipment, rather than marching. Also called the pit. See [Set up the front ensemble](/guides/3d-view/front-ensemble/).

## Front sideline

The sideline nearest the audience. Front and back on the field are measured from it.

## Hash

A row of short marks across each yard line that divides the field front to back. Where the hashes sit depends on the kind of field: high school, college and pro fields put them at different distances from the sidelines.

## Held

A marcher's state on a page where you've chosen to keep it still: a hold keeps it where it last arrived. See [Defined, Held, Not defined](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/).

## Hold

A stretch of counts in which marchers stay still on purpose. See [Make marchers stand still](/guides/timeline/holds/). A held count in the music is a fermata.

## Hold style

What marchers do in the 3D View while they're Held: stand, mark time or high mark time. Each hold can have its own, set from the inspector; a hold without one follows Show settings, as every marcher standing still does, Held or Not defined. See [Stand or mark time on a hold](/guides/timeline/holds/#stand-or-mark-time-on-a-hold).

## Home

The start of the show, before the first move: where every marcher stands at the beginning. On the timeline it's the box with a house. It's numbered as a page, page 0 unless [Show settings](/guides/field/show-settings/#numbers-and-timing) start the numbers elsewhere, and the shortcut list calls it the first page; the page after it is Page 1. See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/#pages-and-their-flags).

## Inspector

The panel at the side of the window that shows the details of what you've selected: the page, or the selected marchers and their positions. See [The window](/guides/workspace/the-window/#the-inspector).

## Interval

The distance from one marcher to the next in a shape, in steps, such as 2 or 2½. A box has one interval across and one front to back. See [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

## Keyframe

A position you set for a marcher at a count. Marchers move from one keyframe to the next. See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/#how-marchers-get-from-one-set-to-the-next).

## Line

A shape that places marchers evenly along a straight line, a line with corners, or a curve. See [Draw a line or curve](/guides/shapes/draw-a-line/).

## Loop

In Expert, a stretch of the show that playback repeats while you work on it. See [Play the show](/guides/timeline/play-the-show/).

## Marcher

Anyone on the field, in any section, including the color guard.

Also called a performer or a dot in other drill software.

## Measure

A group of counts in the music, as numbered in the score. The timeline shows measure numbers above the counts.

## Move

A stretch of time on the timeline in which marchers go from one place to another. A page usually has one move across it; in Expert, a move can also start or end in the middle of a page, or run across several pages. See [Make and change moves](/guides/timeline/moves/).

Also called a transition.

## Music offset

How many seconds after count 1 the music starts, so the first count lines up with the recording. See [Add the show's music](/guides/music/audio/).

## Not defined

A marcher's state on a page where nothing has been decided for it. It shows the last position you set, and moves with any edit to that position. See [Defined, Held, Not defined](/guides/timeline/defined-held-not-defined/).

## Page

A numbered stretch of counts that ends at a set the marchers reach. Pages are numbered as on a drill chart. See [Add and change pages](/guides/timeline/pages/).

## Page's flag

The line at the end of a page on the timeline, where the marchers reach the page's set. The app calls it Page 3's flag, and you can drag it to make the page longer or shorter. See [Add and change pages](/guides/timeline/pages/).

## Pivot

The point a group of marchers or a shape turns around, shown as a small yellow dot. It starts in the middle; you can move it. See [Align, space and turn marchers](/guides/marchers/align-and-space/).

## Playhead

The line on the timeline that marks the count you're looking at. The field shows where every marcher is at that count. See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/#the-playhead-and-the-start-flag).

Also called the cursor or the current count.

## Preview

The view for watching the show without changing it. Its opposite is Editor. See [Watch the show in Preview](/guides/workspace/editor-and-preview/).

## Prop

An object on the field, such as a riser or a backdrop, that you draw and move on the timeline like marchers. See [Add props to the field](/guides/3d-view/props/).

## Readout

The text above the timeline that says where the playhead is, by page and count ("Pg 3 · ct 4/10") and by measure and beat ("m9 beat 4"). See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/).

## Rehearsal mark

A letter or name for a place in the music, such as A or Letter B, shown with the measures. You can go to one by name.

## Section

A group of marchers by instrument or role, such as Trumpet or Color guard. Each section has a prefix for its drill numbers.

## Set

Where the marchers stand at the end of a page: the picture a drill chart shows for that page.

Also called a picture or a drill set.

## Shape

A box, circle, line, curve or arc you draw on the field to place the selected marchers in it. See [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

## Show

One `.dots` file: its marchers, music, field and drill.

Also called a drill file or a project.

## Side 1 and Side 2

The two halves of the field, split at the 50 yard line. Side 1 is on the audience's left and Side 2 on the right, unless the show's field names them differently.

## Snap

Landing on a grid of steps or on the field's lines as you drag. See [Snapping and modifier keys](/guides/shapes/snapping-and-modifier-keys/) and [Move marchers](/guides/marchers/move-marchers/).

## Spot

One place in a shape where a marcher will stand. A shape shows a spot for every selected marcher before you place them. See [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

## Start flag

In Expert, the line on the timeline with a pennant on top that marks where Play starts and where marchers leave from when you move them. In Basic there is no start flag: the start of the page you're on does both jobs. See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/#the-playhead-and-the-start-flag).

## Step size

How far a marcher travels on each count, given as steps per five yards, such as 8 to 5. OpenMarch can mark moves whose steps are too large to march. See [Step size warnings](/guides/field/step-size-warnings/).

## Subset

A page added inside another page and numbered after it with a letter, such as 2A after page 2, so the pages after it keep their numbers. See [Add and change pages](/guides/timeline/pages/#make-a-page-a-subset).

## Tablet layout

The layout OpenMarch uses on a tablet, with bigger controls, a row of buttons over the field and a shorter playback row. See [Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/).

## Tag

A named group of marchers you choose yourself, such as "Soloists", to select them together or give them their own look. See [Change how marchers look](/guides/marchers/appearance/#make-a-tag).

## Tempo

The speed of the music, in beats per minute. A show can change tempo, all at once or gradually. See [Set the tempo to match the music](/guides/music/tempo/).

## Timeline

The strip along the bottom of the window that holds the show's counts, measures, pages, moves and music, with the playback controls. See [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/).

## Venue

Where the 3D View sets the show: a high school or college stadium, a domed pro stadium, a gym or an indoor arena, with its lighting and crowd. See [Change the venue and uniforms](/guides/3d-view/venue-and-uniforms/).

## Yard line

One of the lines across the field every five yards, numbered from each goal line to the 50. Drill positions are often given as steps from the nearest yard line.

The metronome clicks on every count while the show plays. Use it to hear whether the counts sit on the music, and to rehearse a page at its tempo with the music muted.

## Turn the metronome on or off

Press <Kbd action="toggleMetronome" />, or:

- select the speaker button in the timeline's controls, then <Ui k="timeline.sound.metronomeOff" /> (it reads <Ui k="timeline.sound.metronomeOn" /> while it's on);
- in Edit music, select <Ui k="tempoMode.toolbar.metronome" /> in the header;
- in the <Ui k="sidebar.metronome" /> panel in the sidebar, turn on <Ui k="music.onOff" />.

A dot on the speaker button shows the metronome is on.

## Hear only the metronome

Select the speaker button, then the speaker icon in its menu (<Ui k="timeline.sound.mute" />). The music goes silent and the metronome keeps counting. Select it again to bring the music back.

## Change how the metronome sounds

<Steps>

1. Select <Ui k="sidebar.metronome" /> in the sidebar.
2. Change any of these:
   - <Ui k="music.accent" />: the first count of each measure clicks louder.
   - <Ui k="music.onlyClickOnMeasure" />: the metronome clicks only on the first count of each measure.
   - <Ui k="music.volume" />: how loud the click is against the music.
   - <Ui k="music.beatStyle" />: <Ui k="music.standard" />, <Ui k="music.sharp" /> or <Ui k="music.smooth" />.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="music-metronome"
  alt="The Metronome panel: switches for On/Off, Accent first beat and clicks on each measure's first count only, a Volume slider at 50%, and Beat style set to Standard."

Stop playback to change the accent, measure-only clicks and beat style; they can't change while the show plays. The volume slider on the timeline's speaker button turns the music and the metronome up and down together.

The metronome's settings last until you close OpenMarch. Each time you open it, the metronome starts off, at 50% volume, with the first count accented.

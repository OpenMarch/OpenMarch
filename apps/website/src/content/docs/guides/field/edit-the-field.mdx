<InShort>Select <Ui k="sidebar.field" /> in the sidebar, or press <Kbd action="fieldEditMode" />, change the field in the panel's tabs, then select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.done" />.</InShort>

Every show has its own field: its size, its <Term id="step-size">step size</Term>, the lines coordinates count from, and how it looks. Start from the template closest to where you perform, then change what's different about your field or floor.

## Open the Field panel

Besides the sidebar button and the shortcut, you can choose <Ui k="actions.field.editField" /> from the field's right-click menu or the command palette. The panel opens beside the field, and the field is fitted beside it and holds still: you can't pan, zoom or pick marchers until you're done.

<Media
  id="field-inspector"
  alt="The Field panel as it opens: Done and its X, the Setup, Lines and Appearance tabs, and the Setup tab with the template the field is based on, its size and steps, and the field's name."
  maxWidth={480}
/>

The panel has three tabs: <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.setup" /> (the template, size, steps and names), <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.lines" /> (the field's lines, see [Edit the field's lines](/guides/field/field-lines/)) and <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.appearance" /> (markings, the background image and colors). Every change shows on the field at once and is one step to undo.

## Keep or throw away your changes

Select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.done" /> or press <Kbd action="fieldEditDone" /> to keep your changes; opening another sidebar panel keeps them too. To throw them away, close the panel with its X (<Ui k="actions.field.discard" />): the field goes back to how it was when you opened the panel, as one step, so <Kbd action="performUndo" /> brings the changes back.

## Change the size or step size

On the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.setup" /> tab:

- <Ui k="fieldEditMode.size.width" /> and <Ui k="fieldEditMode.size.depth" /> follow the outermost lines. To change them, move those lines on the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.lines" /> tab, or choose another template.
- <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.stepSize" /> is the length of one step, with the drill writers' "8 to 5" under it. It sets the grid and how step sizes are worked out.
- <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.stepSizeWarningThreshold" /> is the longest step before a move is marked as too big; see [Step size warnings](/guides/field/step-size-warnings/).
- In <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.measurementSystem" />, choose <Ui k="fieldProperties.options.imperial" /> for inches or <Ui k="fieldProperties.options.metric" /> for centimeters.

<Aside type="caution">
  Marchers don't move when the field's size or step size changes. They keep
  their place on the drawing, so they can end up somewhere else in steps, or off
  the field. Change the field before you write drill, or check every page after.
</Aside>

## Choose another template

<Steps>

1. On the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.setup" /> tab, under <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.sections.template" />, choose a template from <Ui k="fieldEditMode.template.basedOn" />. A sheet says how the lines and the size change.
2. Leave <Ui k="fieldEditMode.template.keepLook" /> on to keep your colors, labels, image settings and side names, or turn it off to take the template's look too.
3. Select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.template.apply" />.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="field-template"
  alt="The sheet for switching to Indoor 50x70 - 24-inch steps: lines added, removed and renamed, the size before and after, the warning that marchers stay where they are on the canvas, and Keep my look turned on."
  maxWidth={480}
/>

The templates are:

- <Ui k="fieldProperties.fieldTemplate.football" />: High school football field, College football field and Pro football field, each with or without end zones, at 8 to 5 (22.5-inch steps). Each puts the hashes where that level of football does: high school, college (NCAA) or pro (NFL) hashes. For college hashes, choose College football field.
- <Ui k="fieldProperties.fieldTemplate.grid" />: indoor floors of 40 × 60, 45 × 90, 50 × 70, 50 × 80, 50 × 90, 55 × 80 and 60 × 90 feet (60 × 90 is the full-size floor WGI competes on), with 24-inch steps or 6 to 5 steps, and SoundSport fields at 8 to 5 or 6 to 5.

Beside the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.sections.template" /> heading, the panel says how many changes you've made to the template. To go back to the template's lines, select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.template.resetShort" />, then <Ui k="fieldEditMode.template.resetApply" />.

## Use this field in another show

Select <Ui k="field.general.exportField" /> and save the `.fieldots` file. In the other show, select <Ui k="field.general.importField" /> and choose it. The window reloads with the new field. The file doesn't include the field's background image.

## Name the field and its sides

- <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.fieldName" />: the field's name. Until you choose a venue in the 3D View, the 3D View guesses one from the field's name and lines (see [Change the venue and uniforms](/guides/3d-view/venue-and-uniforms/)).
- <Ui k="fieldProperties.sections.sideDescriptions" />: the names of the two sides and their short forms, used in coordinates, such as Side 1 and Side 2, or Stage Right and Stage Left.

## Change the markings and labels

<Media
  id="field-appearance"
  alt="The Field panel's Appearance tab: Markings and labels with the half line X and Y intervals at 4 steps, Edge labels switches for the top, bottom, left and right, and the Yard numbers' distances from the front for the home and away numbers."
  maxWidth={480}
/>

On the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.appearance" /> tab, under <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.sections.markings" />:

- <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.halfLineXInterval" /> and <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.halfLineYInterval" />: draw a half line every so many steps from the center or the front. Leave one empty for none that way. Half lines don't change coordinates.
- <Ui k="fieldEditMode.markings.edgeLabels" />: whether the line names outside the field show on each edge.
- <Ui k="fieldEditMode.markings.yardNumbers" />: how many steps from the front the painted numbers' edges are. Clear the home numbers to paint none.

## Put a picture under the field

Use a floor tarp design or a photo of your field as the background.

<Steps>

1. On the <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.tabs.appearance" /> tab, under <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.sections.image" />, select <Ui k="fieldProperties.buttons.importImage" /> and choose an image file.
2. In <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.conformMethod" />, choose <Ui k="fieldProperties.options.fit" /> to fit the whole image inside the field, or <Ui k="fieldProperties.options.fill" /> to fill the field and let the image's edges run over. The image keeps its shape either way.
3. Drag <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.backgroundImageOpacity" /> to fade it.

</Steps>

Turn off <Ui k="fieldProperties.labels.showBackgroundImage" /> to hide the image, or select <Ui k="fieldEditMode.image.remove" /> to take it out of the show. The panel shows the field's and the image's width-to-height ratios, to help you match them.

## Change the field's colors

Under <Ui k="fieldEditMode.panel.sections.colors" />, change the colors of the field's background, lines and labels, and the default colors of marchers, paths and shapes. Each color has its own reset button, and <Ui k="fieldProperties.buttons.resetTheme" /> puts them all back.

## Change how the field is drawn on your computer

The grid, whether half lines show, and the size and place of marchers' labels aren't part of the field: they're app settings in <Ui k="settings.field" />. The field sets only where its half lines go, with the intervals above. See [Settings](/guides/reference/settings/#field).

A box puts the selected marchers in rows and files, round the edge of a rectangle, or spread evenly inside one. You draw it by dragging from one corner to the other, then change its numbers before you place it.

**Before you start:** select the marchers who go in the box, and put the playhead on the count where they should stand in it, usually the end of a move.

<Steps>

1. In the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.shapesTab" /> tab, select <Ui k="shapes.kinds.block.label" />, or press <Kbd action="shapeBlock" />.

2. Drag on the field from one corner of the box to the opposite corner. A marcher stands on the corner you pressed, and the box picks how many files across suit the shape you drag.

3. Change the box if you need to: drag its handles, or use its floating editor (below).

4. Select <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" />, or press <Kbd action="applyShape" />.

</Steps>

The marchers take their spots, as one step you can undo. Until you place it, nobody moves, and <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> throws the box away.

## Change the box on the field

- Drag a side or corner handle to stretch the box. The rows and files stay the same and the spacing between them changes.
- Drag inside the box, or the dot in its middle, to move it.
- Drag the round knob to turn it. It turns in 15° steps.

The handles, the knob and the editor fade while the pointer is away from the box, so you can see the field under them.

## The floating editor

<Media
  id="shapes-fill-menu"
  alt="The floating editor of an open box, with its Fill menu open below it listing Grid, Outline and Spread, each with a small picture."
/>

The first row of the editor holds the box's numbers and tools:

- The size, such as 4 × 3: how many across and how many deep. Select − or + for one fewer or one more across, or drag the number sideways to change how many across and up or down to change how many deep. With the Short back layout, when the marchers don't fill the last rank, the size says how many spots are short.
- ↔ and ↕: the side-to-side and front-to-back spacing, in steps. The chain button after them locks the two together, so changing one changes the other in proportion.
- **↻** turns the box 90° clockwise (<Kbd action="shapeQuarterTurn" />), and the degrees beside it turn it to any angle. The pivot button picks the point it turns and flips around, from a 3 × 3 grid of its corners, sides and center.
- The two flip buttons mirror it side to side (<Kbd action="flipHorizontal" />) or front to back (<Kbd action="flipVertical" />).
- Expand and Shrink make the box bigger or smaller, keeping its proportions.
- <Ui k="shapes.edit.recalculate.label" /> works out who goes where again (<Kbd action="shapeRecalculate" />). It's greyed out when nothing would change.

To change a number, click it and type, then press <Kbd keys="Enter" />; or drag it. Spacings can be typed as whole steps, halves or fractions: 2, 2.5, 2½, 1⅔ or 5/3. A dragged spacing moves in half steps.

The second row says how the box is filled. Its <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.label" /> menu has three choices:

| Fill                                 | What it does                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.files" plain />   | Rows and files. The <Ui k="shapes.edit.layout.label" plain /> menu beside it lays the block out <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.full" plain />, <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.stagger" plain />, <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.rounded" plain />, <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.shortBack" plain /> or <Ui k="shapes.distribute.layout.notched" plain />, and says how many marchers to add or remove for a layout that can't take this many. |
| <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.outline" plain /> | Round the edge, with a marcher on every corner and the middle empty. When the count doesn't go round evenly, the Leftover menu picks the side that has one fewer marcher, and whether it leaves a gap or spreads its marchers wider. <Ui k="shapes.edit.evenOut.label" plain /> spaces everyone as evenly as the box allows. |
| <Ui k="shapes.edit.mode.spread" plain />  | Evenly over the whole box, in no rows. <Ui k="shapes.edit.reshuffle.label" plain /> gives another even layout in the same box; the arrow beside it shows the layout's <Ui k="shapes.edit.seed.label" plain />, which you can copy, or type back in to get that exact spread again. <Ui k="shapes.edit.edge.label" plain /> stands some marchers along the outline and spreads the rest inside. |

Switching between them keeps the box where you drew it, and each fill remembers its own numbers, so switching back gives you what you had.

## Type a box instead

<Steps>

1. With the marchers selected, press <Kbd action="openCommandBar" /> to open the command bar.

2. Type "box", then press <Kbd keys="Enter" />.

3. Answer each question in the command bar: the fill, the size (such as 6x4), the side-to-side spacing, the front-to-back spacing and the layout. Each answer starts with a default, so <Kbd keys="Enter" /> keeps it. The box shows on the field as you type.

</Steps>

The last answer places the box. You can also type the numbers in one go: "box 6x4 2" is a box 6 across and 4 deep, 2 steps apart. <Kbd keys="Backspace" /> in an empty answer goes back a question, and <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> cancels.

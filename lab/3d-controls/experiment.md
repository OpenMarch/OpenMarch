Open the **3D View** from the **View** tab. The controls are in **View settings**
(the gear button in the 3D View), under **Keyboard and mouse**.

**What to try**

- Drag to move across the field.
- Hold Ctrl, Alt (Option on a Mac) or Shift while you drag to orbit. Dragging
  with the right mouse button orbits too.
- Scroll or pinch to zoom toward the pointer.
- Hold Shift while you scroll to move across the field.
- Change **Controls** to **Trackpad**: scrolling and dragging now orbit, and a
  pinch zooms. Shift+drag or the right mouse button moves across the field.
- Try both with whatever you have: a mouse, a trackpad, or both.

When a marcher's step size exceeds the field's warning threshold, OpenMarch draws that pathway in red (dashed) so you can spot over-threshold transitions. These are **step size warnings**, not errors.

## Editing the threshold

The warning threshold lives on the field, not in the View tab.

1. Open the field picker (field icon) and click **Customize**.
2. On the **General** tab, find **Step Size Warning Threshold**.
3. Enter the maximum step distance you want before a pathway is flagged (inches or centimeters, matching your field's measurement system).

Marchers whose step size exceeds this distance get a warning-colored path. Set the threshold to `0` to disable the warning calculation entirely.

For more on field settings, see [Editing the Field](/guides/editing-the-grid).

## Turning off the red lines

1. Open the **View** tab in the toolbar.
2. Click **Step size warnings** to toggle them off (eye slash).

This control sits next to Previous Paths and Next Paths and uses the same eye / eye-slash pattern. Your choice is saved and persists across reloads.

![Step size warnings toggle on the View tab](/docs/guides/step-size-warnings/view-toggle.png)

With warnings off:

- Over-threshold paths no longer force-show in red.
- Pathways follow normal Previous Paths / Next Paths visibility.
- Visible pathways use normal (non-red) styling.
